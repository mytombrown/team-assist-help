## What it is

- **Tower defense.** On iPhone and iPad.
- **Five rifts.** Each has its own enemies, its own warden and one new tower.
- **Eight towers.** Three upgrade paths each.
- **Endless and a Daily Challenge** once you know the ropes.

## Start playing

- **Tap PLAY.** Pick a rift, then a board.
- **Dust Plains board 1** teaches the game.
- **Tap a square** next to the path. Pick a tower. Its price is on the card.
- **Tap START.** Enemies walk the path to your base.
- **The heart** is your base. An enemy that gets through takes some of it.
- **Base at 0:** the board is lost. Try again.
- **Survive every wave** to pass the board.

## Between waves

- **Up to 2 minutes** to build. The score clock is paused.
- **Tap SKIP** to start the next wave now.
- **Speed:** tap **1x** to switch to 2x or 4x. Tap again to go back.

## Towers

- **Tap a tower** to upgrade it. Three paths, each with its own price.
- **Lvl cap** on a path: raise the tower's level first.
- **Raise a level:** tap **TOWERS** on the title screen, then **MAX TO LEVEL**. It costs XP.
- **New towers:** **BUY TOWER** on the same screen, with XP. A rift's tower opens with that rift.
- **XP:** every wave you survive and every board you pass.
- **Money** is only for the board you are in. It starts fresh each board.

| Tower | Good for |
|---|---|
| Machine Gun | Small fast enemies. Cheap. |
| Mortar Cannon | Packs. Lobs a blast. Takes a 2 by 2 spot. Can't hit flyers. |
| Rocket Launcher | One big target. Cracks armor. |
| Arc Coil | Armor. The bolt jumps between enemies. |
| Flame Tower | Crowds up close. What burns stays down. (Grave Rift) |
| Frost Cannon | Slows everything. Frozen enemies stop healing. (Fang Rift) |
| Railgun | A long line. Goes through shields. (Iron Rift) |
| Flak Tower | Flyers. (Hive Rift) |

## Field reports

- **A new enemy coming?** Commander Vale sends a report between waves.
- **It says** what it is and which tower beats it.
- **Read them again:** **REPORTS** on the title screen.

## The rifts

- **Dust Plains:** aliens. 10 boards.
- **Grave Rift:** zombies. 20 boards.
- **Fang Rift:** monsters. 20 boards.
- **Iron Rift:** soldiers. 20 boards.
- **Hive Rift:** giant bugs. 20 boards.
- **Pass a rift's last board** to open the next one.
- **Bosses** at board 10 and board 20 of each rift.

## Endless

- **Opens** after Dust Plains.
- **Enemies from every rift you've opened**, all mixed.
- **No last wave.** Each one is harder, until your base falls.
- **Score:** every kill's bounty times the wave it fell in.
- **Top 10 runs** on this phone.

## Daily Challenge

- **One board a day.** The same board for everyone.
- **Turns over at midnight**, US Central time.
- **Your towers**, plus that rift's tower lent at level 1.
- **Try as often as you like.** Today's top 10 on this phone.

## Looks

- **Same boards, towers and enemies.** Only the art changes.
- **Pick one** under **CHOOSE YOUR LOOK** on the title screen. Slide the row.
- **Rift:** modern 3D, real light.
- **16-Bit:** chunky pixels, like a 90s console.
- **Classic:** big flat blocks, like the first home consoles.
- **Vector:** glowing lines, like an 80s arcade.
