## No sound

- **Check the silent switch** and the volume.

## A board is too hard

- **Read Vale's report.** It names the tower that beats the new enemy.
- **Build near bends.** Enemies stay in range longer.
- **Kill Bloaters early**, far from your base. They burst into Crawlers.
- **Spend your XP** on the **TOWERS** screen. Higher levels open more upgrades.
- **Replay an earlier board** for more XP.

## A rift is locked

- **Pass the last board** of the rift before it.

## Endless is locked

- **Pass Dust Plains** first.

## An upgrade says "Lvl cap"

- **The tower's level** stops that path.
- **Raise it:** **TOWERS** on the title screen, then **MAX TO LEVEL**.

## A tower says "OPENS WITH" a rift

- **Open that rift first.** Then buy the tower with XP.

## The phone gets warm at 4x

- **Tap the speed button** to go back to 2x or 1x.

## Contact support

- **Email** [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).
- **Say** your phone, the rift and board, and what you tapped.
