## No sound

- **Check the silent switch** and the volume. The game follows the silent switch.
- **Check the music and sound toggles** in Bag · Team.

## A fight is too hard

- **Level up first.** Do side quests and the Training Ring.
- **Stock up** on Juice Boxes and Paint Pots.
- **Guard** when a boss warns of a big attack.

## I don't know where to go

- **Check the clues count** at the top.
- **Talk to everyone** in Craftville.

## My save is gone

- **The save lives on this phone.** It does not move to a new phone.
- **Deleting the app deletes the save.**

## Contact support

- **Email** [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).
- **Say** your phone, where you are in the game, and what you tapped.

## For grown-ups

- **We collect nothing.** Nothing leaves the phone.
- **Help** opens this site only after a grown-up check.
