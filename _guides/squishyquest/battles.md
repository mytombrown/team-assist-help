## Your first fight

- **It coaches you.** A tip card says which button to tap.
- **One button at a time:** Splat, then Guard, then Items if Pip is hurt, then Splat.
- **After that, you choose.**

## Your turn

- **Each fighter has a speed bar.** When a hero's bar fills, it is their turn.
- **The bars wait** while you choose.
- **Pick one:**
  - **Splat** — a paint attack. Then tap an enemy.
  - **Skills** — special moves. They cost **PP** (paint points).
  - **Items** — Juice Box, Paint Pot, Glitter Pop. Then tap a friend.
  - **Guard** — take half the hit, get 3 PP back.
  - **Run** — leave the fight. Not from a boss.

## Skills

- **Pip:** Rainbow Burst at level 3, Cheer at level 7.
- **Victoria:** Shield Bash, then Cover at level 4.
- **No skills yet?** Level up.

## Items

- **Juice Box** — heals HP.
- **Paint Pot** — refills 20 PP.
- **Glitter Pop** — wakes a fallen friend.
- **Buy them** in Craftville with buttons.

## Winning

- **Victory!** gives XP and buttons.
- **LEVEL UP!** shows any new skill.
- **A friend is "Bleached!"?** A Glitter Pop wakes them.

## Stats and weapons

- **Five stats:** HP, PP, Strength, Defense, Speed.
- **See them:** Bag · Team → tap a hero.
- **Weapons:** tap one in the hero's profile to swap.
- **Each weapon is good at something different:**
  - Paint Brush — Strength (Pip starts with it).
  - Foam Sword — Defense (Victoria starts with it).
  - Spray Can — Speed.
  - Glitter Wand — PP.
  - Sponge Mallet — HP.
  - Big Roller — big Strength, a bit slower.
  - Super Marker — Penny's reward. Strength + PP.
- **Buy them** at Penny's Paint Shop.
