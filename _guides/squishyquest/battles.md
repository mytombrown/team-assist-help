## Your first fight

- **It coaches you.** A tip card says which button to tap.
- **One button at a time:** Splat, then Guard, then Items if Pip is hurt, then Splat.
- **After that, you choose.**

## Your turn

- **Each fighter has a speed bar.** When a hero's bar fills, it is their turn.
- **The bars wait** while you choose.
- **Pick one:**
  - **Splat** — a paint attack. Then tap an enemy.
  - **Skills** — special moves. They cost **PP** (paint points).
  - **Items** — Juice Box, Paint Pot, Glitter Pop. Then tap a friend.
  - **Guard** — take half the hit, get 3 PP back.
  - **Run** — leave the fight. Not from a boss.

## Tap the ring

- **A ring closes on the enemy** when you attack.
- **Tap when it turns green.** "Great!" hits harder.
- **Tap a little early** and you still get "Good!"
- **No tap?** A normal hit. Nothing is lost.
- **When an enemy swings,** a ring closes on your hero. Tap on green to **block** some of the hit.
- **Great taps in a row** count up next to the ★.

## The star gauge and super moves

- **The star gauge** is at the top right.
- **It fills** when your team lands hits and when it takes hits.
- **It keeps what it has** from one fight to the next.
- **Full?** A bright **★** button shows on that hero's turn. Tap it for their super move.
- **Every hero has one:**
  - Pip — Rainbow Cannon. Blasts every enemy.
  - Victoria — Mega Bash. One giant hit.
  - Sunny — Sunny Day. Everyone up and healed.
  - Nika — Paint Tornado. Hits and slows every enemy.
- **New friends** bring their own.
- **Using it empties the gauge.**

## Who goes next

- **The turn bar** is at the top left.
- **The faces** show who acts next, soonest first.

## Sneak attacks

- **Enemies can spot you** on the map. A red **!** pops up and they chase.
- **You can outrun them.**
- **Walk slowly** (push the stick lightly) and they don't notice.
- **Touch one that hasn't noticed:** "Sneak attack!" Your whole team goes first.

## Skills

- **Pip:** Rainbow Burst at level 3, Cheer at level 7.
- **Victoria:** Shield Bash, then Cover at level 4.
- **No skills yet?** Level up.

## Items

- **Juice Box** — heals HP.
- **Paint Pot** — refills 20 PP.
- **Glitter Pop** — wakes a fallen friend.
- **Buy them** in Craftville with buttons.

## Winning

- **Victory!** gives XP and buttons.
- **Sometimes enemies drop** a Juice Box or a Paint Pot. It goes in your Bag.
- **LEVEL UP!** shows any new skill.
- **A friend is "Bleached!"?** A Glitter Pop wakes them.

## Stats and weapons

- **Five stats:** HP, PP, Strength, Defense, Speed.
- **See them:** Bag · Team → tap a hero.
- **Weapons:** tap one in the hero's profile to swap.
- **Each weapon is good at something different:**
  - Paint Brush — Strength (Pip starts with it).
  - Foam Sword — Defense (Victoria starts with it).
  - Spray Can — Speed.
  - Glitter Wand — PP.
  - Sponge Mallet — HP.
  - Big Roller — big Strength, a bit slower.
  - Super Marker — Penny's reward. Strength + PP.
- **Buy them** at Penny's Paint Shop.

## Weapons in Rainbow Skies (Chapter 2)

- **Kip's Kite Shop** in Skyport sells a stronger one of each kind:
  - Kite Brush — Strength.
  - Cloud Shield — Defense.
  - Wind Spray — Speed.
  - Rainbow Wand — PP.
  - Puffy Mallet — HP.
- **Sunny** brings her Glow Staff — HP + PP.
- **The Sunbeam Brush** is hidden in the Storm Factory. It's stronger than anything in the shop.
- **Sun beats rain:** it hits storm foes extra hard. Bring it to Captain Drizzle.

## Weapons in Spin Cycle (Chapter 3)

- **Bobbin's Button Bin** in Sudsville sells a stronger one of each kind:
  - Needle Brush — Strength.
  - Ironing Shield — Defense.
  - Spin Spray — Speed.
  - Bubble Wand — PP.
  - Pillow Mallet — HP.
- **Nika** brings her Swirl Brush — PP + Strength. Her Color Storm hits every enemy.
- **The Permanent Marker** is hidden in the Dryer Drum. It won't wash out: it hits wash foes extra hard. Bring it to Lady Lint.

## Four fight at once (Chapters 4–10)

- **Pip always fights,** plus three friends.
- **A new friend takes the 4th spot.** The one they replace rests on the bench.
- **The bench still gets XP,** so everyone keeps up.
- **Swap who fights:** Bag · Team → tap a bench friend → pick who they replace.

## New friends

- **Pengu** (Ch 4) — Belly Slide hits everyone; Snow Fort guards the team.
- **Owl** (Ch 5) — Wise Eye, one huge hit; Hoot slows every enemy.
- **Axo** (Ch 6) — Splash Heal heals everyone; Bubble Guard shields one friend.
- **Dragon** (Ch 7) — Toasty Breath hits everyone; Wing Gust knocks enemies back.
- **Piggy** (Ch 8) — Snack Time heals and refills paint; Belly Bounce knocks back.
- **Uni** (Ch 9) — Rainbow Horn, big magic; Starshine, a big heal.
- **Pell** (Ch 10) — Sketch Strike, four quick hits; Color Fill refills everyone's paint.

## Learning as you go (Chapter 1)

- **The first time** you win, level up, learn a skill, find a clue, get a teammate, shop or meet a boss, a short card explains it.
- **After a fight,** if anyone's HP or PP is under half, a card says how to fix it.
