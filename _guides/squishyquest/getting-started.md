## What it is

- **A turn-based adventure** on iPhone. Hold the phone sideways.
- **Craftville's colors are draining away.** Pip finds out who did it.
- **Chapter 1, Paint Park,** is in this test build. More lands are coming.
- **No ads. No accounts. No purchases.**

## Start playing

- **Tap New Game.**
- **Already playing?** Tap **Continue**. It shows the place, Pip's level and play time.
- **New Game with a save** asks first. **Keep my save** backs out.

## Walk and talk

- **Drag anywhere** for a thumb-stick. It sits under your thumb.
- **Tap the ground** to walk there.
- **Tap a person,** or the action button, to talk or use something.
- **Crayons, spots and clues** pick up when you walk over them.
- **Map** at an exit takes you to the next place.

## The clues

- **Find 3 clues.** The count shows at the top: **Clues 0/3**.
- **Each clue opens the next place.**
- **All 3** open the Bleach Works.

## Saving

- **Bag · Team → Save.**
- **It also saves** as you walk, when you enter a place, and when you leave the app.
