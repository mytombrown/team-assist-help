## Keep a quest in the corner

- **Bag · Team → Quests.**
- **Tap any quest,** main or side. It shows in the top corner while you walk.
- **The main quest** says what to do next.
- **A finished quest** leaves the corner; the main quest comes back.

## Side quests in Paint Park

- **Lost Crayons** — find the crayons hidden around Paint Park for the art teacher.
- **Juice Run** — take juice to people around town.
- **Paint the Town** — splat bleached spots back to color. Penny gives you the Super Marker.
- **Fort Training Ring** — extra fights against Cardboard Knights.
- **A lost sock** — it goes in your Bag. Its quest starts in Spin Cycle.

## Side quests in Rainbow Skies

- **Lost Kites** — find Kip's six kites around the sky.
- **Rainbow Puddles** — splat Dot's eight grey puddles back to rainbow.
- **Sky Mail** — Flap's three letters: Professor Nimbus, Aunt Breeze, and Glim.

## Side quests in Spin Cycle

- **The Lost Sock** — Granny Purl wants both striped socks. One is in Glue Woods, one on Hamper Hill.
- **Lost Buttons** — find Bobbin's eight buttons.
- **Stain Splat** — splat Fluff's ten grey stains.
- **Laundry Run** — Mama Foam's four bundles: Mayor Wringer, Granny Purl, Bobbin, and Old Spool in the Lint Caves.
- **Spin Ring** — three rounds of fights in Sudsville.

## Side quests in Chapters 4–10

- **Every land has four:** find 8 lost things, splat 10 grey spots, a 4-stop delivery run, and a 3-round ring.
- **4 Frosty Flight** — Lost Mittens, Thaw Spots, Cocoa Run, Ice Rink Ring.
- **5 Thunder Tops** — Lost Bulbs, Scorch Marks, Umbrella Run, Thunder Ring.
- **6 Pirate Cove** — Lost Doubloons, Paper Patches, Bottle Post, Pirate Ring.
- **7 Volcano Valley** — Cool Crystals, Ash Spots, Water Run, Lava Ring.
- **8 Candy Coast** — Lost Sprinkles, Gum Spots, Cake Run, Candy Ring.
- **9 Cosmic Coast** — Lost Stars, Dark Spots, Moon Mail, Star Ring.
- **10 The Blank Page** — Lost Colors, Blank Spots, Camp Run, Last Ring.

## Why do them

- **Items and XP.** A stronger team for the boss.
- **All optional.** The story goes on without them.

## Shops

- **Juice Stand** and **Paint Shop** in Craftville.
- **Kip's Kite Shop** and **Aunt Breeze's Inn** in Skyport.
- **Bobbin's Button Bin** and **Mama Foam's Laundromat Inn** in Sudsville.
- **Every town from Chapter 4 on** has an inn (rest, save, snacks) and a weapon shop.
- **Pay with buttons** from battles.
