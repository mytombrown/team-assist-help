## Side quests in Paint Park

- **Lost Crayons** — find the crayons hidden around Paint Park for the art teacher.
- **Juice Run** — take juice to people around town.
- **Paint the Town** — splat bleached spots back to color.
- **Fort Training Ring** — extra fights against Cardboard Knights.
- **A lost sock** — keep it.

## Why do them

- **Items and XP.** A stronger team for the boss.
- **All optional.** The story goes on without them.

## Shops

- **Juice Stand** and **Paint Shop** in Craftville.
- **Pay with buttons** from battles.
