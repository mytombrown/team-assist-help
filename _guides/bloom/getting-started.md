## What it is

- **A garden puzzle.** On iPhone and iPad.
- **Plant the seeds. Grow the picture.**
- **Daily garden:** one new garden every day.

## Start playing

- **Put your seeds** on the plots.
- **Red poppies** spread in a +.
- **Blue cornflowers** spread in an ×.
- **Yellow sunflowers** jump two plots.
- **Tap the watering can.** Everything grows at once.
- **Two colors on one plot** make pebbles. Nothing grows there.
- **Match the picture** on the seed packet to clear the garden.

## Hearts and coins

- **Try again** costs a heart. Your seeds stay where they are.
- **Up to 5 hearts.** One comes back every 30 minutes.
- **Coins come from playing.** Hint: 30 coins. Extra seed: 50 coins.

## The shop

- **Coin packs:** Handful, Basket, Wheelbarrow, Greenhouse.
- **Starter Bundle:** coins, 5 hints, 3 extra seeds. One time only.
- **1 Hour Endless Hearts.** Buy again to add an hour.
- **New device?** Tap **Restore purchases** in the shop.

## Scores

- **Game Center is optional.** Signed in, your scores go on the boards and trophies show as achievements.
