## Trail Game

- **1 or 2 players.** Pick both on the home screen first.
- **Tap or flick the die.** Solve the problem to hop along.
- **Reach the finish** to end the level. **Next Level** brings more spaces and bigger numbers.
- **Stop** ends the game. Points become coins.

## Flashcards

- **One card at a time.** Pick the answer.
- **5 right in a row** steps up a level. **3 wrong in a row** steps down.
- **Right** adds points. **Wrong** takes some off.
- **After a wrong answer:** on devices with Apple Intelligence, a hint says the problem another way.
- **Done** ends the round.

## Story Trail

- **Flashcards told as tiny stories.** Your child's squishy is the hero.
- **The app picks the numbers.** On devices with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model writes the words. Otherwise a built-in set of stories is used.

## Squishy Match

- **20 levels** on the **Pop Meadow** map.
- **Make the number at the top.** Drag through squishies that add up to it.
- **Each level has a move limit** and up to 3 stars.
- **The −1 bubble** takes one off a squishy's number.

## Number Slide

- **Swipe** up, down, left or right. Every squishy slides.
- **Two the same join** into the next number. 2 + 2 = 4.
- **Every join earns a coin.** Bigger squishies earn bonus coins.
- **No more moves!** ends the game. **Play again** or **Done**.
- **How to play** explains it again.

## Squishy Fishing

- **Tap Fish!** to cast.
- **Answer the math** to reel it in. A wrong answer: **It got away!**
- **Junk and snacks pay more than fish.**
- **A Golden Prize** pays bonus coins.

## Temp Chamber

- **Two thermometers:** Celsius and Fahrenheit. Slide either one. The other follows.
- **Cold:** the squishy freezes. **Hot:** it melts.

## Playroom

- **Free for everyone:** tap it to squish, rub it to tickle, press **Dance mode**.
- **What can I do?** lists everything it does.
