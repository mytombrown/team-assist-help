## The tiles are grey

- **No player is picked.** Tap a squishy at the top first.
- **No players yet:** the screen says **Make a player to start!** Tap **New**.

## A tile says "1 player"

- **Two players are picked.** Only the **Trail Game** plays two.
- **Tap one player** to unpick them.

## Players or coins are gone

- **Players are saved on this device only.** They do not move to a new device.
- **Deleting the app deletes the players.**
- **The Squishy Pack does come back.** See below.

## The Squishy Pack is missing

- **Tap the bag** under a player, then **Restore Purchases**.
- **Use the same Apple ID** that bought it.
- **"No Squishy Pack on this Apple ID"**: this Apple ID did not buy it.

## A purchase didn't finish

- **"Waiting for approval"**: Ask to Buy is on. The family organizer approves it.
- **"Purchase failed"**: try again.
- **"The store is not ready yet"**: wait a moment, then try again.
- **"Could not check the purchase"**: tap **Restore Purchases**.

## Windy or Boo! do nothing

- **"Windy and Boo! need a grown-up to allow the microphone."** Turn the trick on again and pass the grown-up check.
- **"The microphone is off. A grown-up can turn it on in Settings."** Open Settings › Squishy Math › Microphone and turn it on.

## No sound or no voice

- **Check the speaker button** on the home screen.
- **Check the voice button.** It is per player, and starts off for ages 7 and over.
- **Check the silent switch** and the volume.

## The math is too easy or too hard

- **Check the age:** bag under the player › **Change age**.
- **In Learn:** tap **Check again (1 minute)**.
