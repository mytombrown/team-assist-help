## What it is

- **A math game for kids 4 to 12.** On iPhone and iPad.
- **Each child is a player.** Their age sets which math they get.
- **No ads, no accounts.** Players are saved on this device only.

## Make a player

- **Tap New** (the **+** in the row of squishies at the top).
- **Type a name** under **What's your name?**
- **Pick an age** under **How old are you?** (4 to 12).
- **Pick a squishy** and **Pick a color.**
- **Tap Let's go!**

## The math by age

| Age | Math |
|---|---|
| 4–5 | + |
| 6–7 | + − |
| 8–9 | + − × |
| 10–12 | + − × ÷ |

- **Change it later:** tap the bag under the player, then **Change age**. Coins, badges and levels stay.

## The home screen

- **Pick a player first.** Tap their squishy. The prompt says **Who's playing? Pick 1 or 2**.
- **Two players:** only the **Trail Game** plays two. The other tiles show **· 1 player** and use the first one picked.
- **The tiles:** **Trail Game**, **Flashcards**, **Story Trail**, **Learn**, **Squishy Match**, **Playroom**, **Temp Chamber**, **Number Slide**, **Squishy Fishing**.
- **Speaker button:** turns game sounds on or off.
- **Voice button:** turns the squishy's talking on or off for the picked player. It starts off for players 7 and over, on under 7.
- **Bag under a player:** opens their **Shop** and **Achievements**.
