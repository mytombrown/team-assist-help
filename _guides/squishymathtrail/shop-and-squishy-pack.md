## Open a player's page

- **Tap the bag** under a player on the home screen.
- **Two tabs:** **Shop** and **Achievements**.
- **Done** closes it.

## Coins and the shop

- **Every game earns coins.** Each player has their own.
- **Shop** spends them on **Squishies**, **Colors** and **Tricks**.
- **Owned** looks can be worn any time. **Wearing** marks the current one.

## Achievements

- **Achievements** shows badges, stats and the **Next badge**.
- **Each badge pays bonus coins.**

## The Squishy Pack

- **One purchase for this Apple ID.** It covers every player on the device.
- **It opens** 9 more squishies, 5 more colors and 15 tricks to buy with coins.
- **It also gives** 5,000 coins to every player.
- **Price:** shown on the button, **Unlock all for …**. The App Store sets it.

## The grown-up check

- **Before any purchase**, **Ask a grown-up** appears.
- **A sum is written in words.** Type the answer in numbers.
- **Tap Continue.** Then Apple's purchase sheet opens.
- **Wrong answer:** **Not quite — try again** and a new sum.
- **Cancel** backs out. Nothing is bought.

## Restore Purchases

- **On the Squishy Pack card**, tap **Restore Purchases**.
- **Use it on a new device** or after reinstalling. Sign in with the same Apple ID.
- **Success:** **Squishy Pack restored!**

## Tricks and the microphone

- **Tricks cost 2,000 coins each.** Squishy Pack only.
- **Each trick has an on/off switch** under **Tricks**.
- **Windy and Boo! use the microphone.** A grown-up check comes first, then iPhone asks for the microphone.
- **It only measures how loud it is.** Nothing is recorded.

## Delete a player

- **Tap Delete player** at the bottom, then **Yes, delete**.
- **Coins, badges and squishies go too.** This cannot be undone.
