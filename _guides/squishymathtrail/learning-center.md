## Open it

- **Pick a player** on the home screen.
- **Tap Learn.**

## The one-minute check

- **First visit:** the **One-minute check** runs.
- **Tap Start!** to begin, or **Not now** to skip it.
- **It only places your child.** It finds a starting level in each skill.
- **At the end:** **All done!** shows a level per skill. Tap **To the Learning Center**.
- **Run it again** any time: **Check again (1 minute)**.

## The worlds

- **Pick a skill to learn.** Each skill is a world. The squishy wears a costume.

| World | Skill | From age |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Orchard | Counting | 4 |
| Balloon Fair | Adding | 4 |
| Building Site | Shapes | 4 |
| Pirate Cove | Taking away | 6 |
| Chocolate Factory | Fractions | 7 |
| Cookie Bakery | Times | 8 |
| Mystery Manor | Missing numbers and equations | 8 |
| Rocket Range | Decimals | 9 |
| Pizza Party | Dividing | 10 |

## A lesson

- **The squishy explains it.** Your child taps the picture to go on.
- **Then 5 questions.**
- **4 right** moves that skill up one level. There are 12 levels per world.
- **A lesson never moves a child down.**
- **A wrong answer** shows the working for that question.
- **Back to skills** returns to the worlds.

## Learning badges

- **Learning badges** at the bottom show what is earned and the next one to get.
- **Each badge pays coins.**

## How the games use it

- **The Trail Game and Flashcards** size their numbers to your child's levels.
