## Free plays

- **Free**: every game, 5 plays an hour.
- **No ads. Ever.**
- **A play** is starting a practice round: a game tile, **One more**, **Practice** on the daily card, or **Play** on Ranks.
- **It's used when the round starts**, not when it ends.
- **The daily challenge and Versus never use a play.**
- **Each play comes back** one hour after you used it.
- **The Games tab shows** plays left and when the next one returns.
- **Out of plays?** The sheet offers the daily, Versus, or One More+.

## One More+

- **Unlimited plays.**
- **Price**: $1.99 a month or $9.99 a year.
- **Renews automatically.** Cancel any time in iPhone Settings.
- **Still free for everyone**: ranks, achievements, leaderboards and earned styles.

## Manage it

- **Settings**: the gear on the Games tab.
- **It shows** your One More+ status, **Manage Subscription** and **Restore Purchases**.
