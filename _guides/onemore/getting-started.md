One More is twelve short puzzle and tap games, plus two new ones in 1.2 (TestFlight). Every round pays points, earns achievements and moves you up the ranks.

![Home screen with the daily challenge and the games]({{ '/assets/onemore/01-home.png' | relative_url }})

## The four tabs

- **Games** tab: tap any game to play a practice round. The tile shows your best.
- **Daily challenge**: the purple card on top of the Games tab. One board for everyone, one try.
- **Versus** tab: pick a game, pick a rival, one round each on the same board.
- **Ranks** tab: your rank, the leaderboards, and a row into **Achievements**.
- **Style** tab: tile colors, backgrounds, win effects and titles you have earned.

## After a round

- **One more**: starts another round right away.
- **Board**: opens that game's leaderboard.
