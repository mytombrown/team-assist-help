## What saves where

- **Your bests, points, achievements and rank** save on your phone and to your own iCloud.
- **A new phone** on the same Apple Account picks them up.
- **Style choices** are kept per phone.

## Game Center

- **World leaderboards, level boards and online versus** use Game Center.
- **Sign in** under iPhone Settings › Game Center.

## Without Game Center

- **Still works**: every game, the daily challenge, achievements, unlocks and same-phone versus.
