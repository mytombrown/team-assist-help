## How to Play cards

- **New in 1.2 — TestFlight.**
- **Tap ?** on a game. The round pauses while the card is open.
- **The first round** of each game shows it once.
- **Settings** has every game's card too.
- **Untimed games** get a 90-second clock in versus, so a match never stalls.

## Stackit

![Stackit in play]({{ '/assets/onemore/02-stackit.png' | relative_url }})

- **Drag pieces** from the tray onto the 8×8 board.
- **Fill a whole row or column** to clear it.
- **1 point** per square placed.
- **Clears pay** 10 × lines² × your streak. Clear two at once, or keep clearing turn after turn.
- **Ends** when no piece fits.
- **Clear a line** and its points float up from the board (+N).

## Way Out

- **60 seconds.**
- **Tap an arrow** and it slides off the board — unless another arrow is in its way.
- **10 points** per arrow, **100** per board cleared.
- **A blocked arrow** costs one of your 3 lives.
- **Every board** can be cleared.

## Quick Tap

- **About 30 seconds.**
- **Circles pop up** one at a time. Tap them before they shrink away.
- **Up to 100 points** per circle — faster pays more.
- **Grape circles with an ✕** are decoys: −50.
- **Circles get quicker** as the round goes.

## Doubles

- **Swipe** to slide every tile.
- **Two of the same number** merge into their double.
- **Each merge** pays the new tile's value.
- **Ends** when no swipe can move anything.

## Triples

- **120 seconds.**
- **Tap a tile** nothing is resting on to drop it in the 7-slot tray.
- **Three alike** clear.
- **30 points** per triple, **200** per board.
- **Seven in the tray** with no match ends the round.

## Swap 3

- **20 moves.**
- **Swipe a gem** into its neighbor to line up three or more of one color.
- **10 points** per gem. Chains multiply (×2, ×3…).
- **A swap that makes no line** bounces back and costs no move.
- **Watch it play**: gems slide, matches pop, the rest fall, new ones drop in. Each chain step shows its points.

## Five Letters

- **150 seconds.**
- **Guess** the five-letter word in six tries.
- **Green**: right letter, right spot.
- **Yellow**: in the word, wrong spot.
- **Dark**: not in the word.
- **Solved**: 600 for the first guess, 100 less for each extra guess, + 2 per second left.
- **Not solved**: 0.

## Tower

- **A block slides** back and forth. Tap anywhere to drop it.
- **What hangs over** falls off.
- **10 points** per floor.
- **Land it dead on** for a **Perfect**: full width kept, +15 per perfect in a row (up to 5).
- **Miss the tower** and it's over.

## One Line

- **75 seconds.**
- **Start on the star** and drag one line through every open square, without crossing.
- **100 points** per puzzle + 5 per square.
- **Drag back** to undo.
- **Boards grow** every three you solve.

## Pair Up

- **120 seconds.** Mahjong-style.
- **Tap two matching free tiles** to clear them.
- **Free** means nothing on top and open on the left or right.
- **20 points** per pair, **300** per board.
- **Every board** can be cleared.
- **Run out of pairs?** It reshuffles for −50.

## Pour

- **120 seconds.**
- **Tap a tube**, then where to pour.
- **Colors only pour** onto the same color or an empty tube.
- **25 points** per color in each puzzle you sort.
- **More colors** as you go.
- **Start over** resets the puzzle.

## Mini Sudoku

- **180 seconds.**
- **Fill the 6×6 grid** so 1–6 appear once in every row, column and shaded box.
- **200 points** per puzzle.
- **A wrong number** is refused and costs 20.
- **Every puzzle** has exactly one answer.

## Tic-Tac-Toe

- **New in 1.2 — TestFlight.** Not in the App Store version yet.
- **Pick Classic or Ultimate** with the toggle, then who you're playing.
- **Classic**: one 3×3 board. Three in a row wins. X goes first.
- **Ultimate**: nine small boards in a 3×3.
- **The square you play** sends your rival to that small board (it lights up).
- **That board already won or full?** They can play in any open board.
- **Win three small boards in a row** to win. A drawn small board counts for nobody.
- **Vs the phone**: Easy, Medium or Hard. Classic Hard plays perfectly — you can't beat it, but a draw pays.
- **Who starts** swaps each game.
- **Points vs the phone**: Classic win 50 / 150 (Easy / Medium), draw vs Hard 150. Ultimate win 120 / 350 / 800.
- **Same phone**: two players take turns. Pays no points.
- **Online versus**: Game Center, one move per turn on the same board. You get a notice when it's your move.
- **Online win** pays 150 (Classic) or 400 (Ultimate) and counts as a versus win.
- **Vs the phone and same phone** use a play. Online versus never does.
- **No daily or leaderboard** for this one.

## Crossword

- **New in 1.2 — TestFlight.** Not in the App Store version yet.
- **Type a theme** or a phrase — "Disney princesses", "pizza toppings" — and get a crossword about it.
- **Apple Intelligence** writes the answers and clues on your iPhone. One More builds and checks the grid.
- **Easy or Hard**: pick it under the theme. Your pick is remembered.
- **Easy**: plain clues that name the thing.
- **Hard**: trickier but fair.
- **Green = right, red = wrong.** Free — no time added.
- **Easy** checks each letter as you type it.
- **Hard** checks once a word is full: a right word turns green, its wrong letters turn red.
- **Every square filled but not solved?** A red bar says how many words aren't right yet.
- **Tap a square or a clue**, then type. Tap the same square again to switch Across ↔ Down.
- **Clues** lists them all.
- **Hints**: **Check** clears wrong letters (+10 s). Reveal a letter or word (+15 s per letter).
- **25 points** per word, plus a time bonus — faster scores more. No hints: +150.
- **Making a crossword** needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or newer).
- **Any iPhone** can solve one a friend sends in Versus.
- **Words and clues** stay family-friendly. If a theme can't be used, you'll be asked for another.
- **Versus**: your rival gets the exact same puzzle, same Easy or Hard. Faster time wins — hints add time. 10-minute clock.
- **No world leaderboard**, and never the daily — everyone's puzzle is different.
