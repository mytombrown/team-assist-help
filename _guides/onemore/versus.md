## Start a match

- **Pick a game** on the **Versus** tab, then pick who you're playing.
- **One round each**, on the same board. Higher score wins.
- **Versus is always free.** It never uses a play.

## Who you can play

- **Someone you've played before.**
- **A Game Center friend**: **Show my friends**.
- **Anyone**: Game Center finds a rival.
- **Same phone**: pass it back and forth.
- **Text a friend** (new in 1.2 — TestFlight build 12): play your round, then text them the link. They tap it, One More opens, and they play your board. One link, one friend.
- **Anyone, and someone's already waiting?** You'll see "… is waiting" — play their board too.
- **Friend not in your list?** Game Center only shows friends who have One More and allowed it. Text them a link instead.

## Online timing

- **You play now**, they play when they can.
- **Nobody has to be online** at the same time.

## Your matches

- **Sorted**: **Your turn** first (the number on the Versus tab), then **Waiting on them**, then **Finished**.
- **Finished** shows the latest 3. **See all** for the rest.
- **Rematch**: when a match ends, both players see a rematch button on that match — same game, new board.
- **Rivals**: switch to **Rivals** for one line per person with your record (wins–losses). Tap one for your matches with them, a rematch, or a new match.
- **Finished matches tidy away** after 7 days. Your wins, losses and rival records stay.

## Remove a match

- **New in 1.2 — TestFlight build 12.**
- **Tap the trash** on its row.
- **Finished or nobody joined**: it just goes.
- **Someone's already playing you**: it counts as your loss and their win.

## Scoring rules

- **Untimed games** (Stackit, Doubles, Swap 3, Tower) get a 90-second clock in versus.
- **Online versus scores count** on the game boards — your round goes on that game's Game Center board and your best.
- **Same-phone versus** doesn't count.
- **Tic-Tac-Toe is different** (new in 1.2 — TestFlight): you take turns on one board, one move each turn. See [Tic-Tac-Toe]({{ '/onemore/how-to-play-the-games/' | relative_url }}).
