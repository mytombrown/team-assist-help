## What it is

- **A one-screen puzzle.** iPhone.
- **6 by 6 board.** Three tiles in your tray at a time.
- **Works with no internet.** No ads.

## Place a tile

- **Drag** a tile from the tray onto a square.
- **Or tap it**, then tap a square.
- **Tap players:** the next tile picks itself up.

## Pop

- **Three of a color** in one row or column pop.
- **Gaps are fine.** Empty squares between them still count.
- **A tile in the way** blocks it. No pop.
- **Pops in a row** make a chain. A chain scores more.

## Strays

- **Every 3 tiles,** a stray lands.
- **The ring** shows where the next one lands.
- **Level up:** more strays.

## Game over

- **Board full** ends the game.
- **Play again** starts a new one.
- **Best** is your top score on this phone.
- **New game** mid-game asks first. Tap it again to start over.
- **Close the app mid-game?** The game is still there when you come back.

## How to play, again

- **Tap ?** at the top. Three moves you make yourself.

## Looks and sound

- **Look:** Clean, Glossy or Glowing. Pick one at the bottom. It is remembered.
- **Each look** has its own sounds.
- **Sound button** at the top turns sound off and on.
