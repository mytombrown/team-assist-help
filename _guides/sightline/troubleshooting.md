## No sound

- **Check the silent switch** and the volume.
- **Check the sound button** at the top of the screen.
- **Build 5 and older:** sound could stop after you lock and unlock the phone. Fixed in build 6.

## Three in a line did not pop

- **A tile of another color** sits between them.
- **Rows and columns only.** Not corners.

## A tile will not go down

- **That square is taken.** Pick an empty one.

## A tile is stuck in the air

- **Drag the tile again** from the tray. It goes back and plays on.
- **Build 5 and older:** a tile let go off the squares can stay floating. Fixed in build 6.

## No leaderboards

- **Sign in to Game Center:** Settings, Game Center.
- **Today's Board** is empty until someone plays today's board.

## My best score is not on my other phone

- **Same Apple ID** on both phones.
- **iCloud on:** Settings, your name, iCloud.
- **Give it a minute.** Then open Sight Line again.

## I bought Sight Line Full but it is locked

- **Open the card:** tap Endless.
- **Tap Restore purchase.** Same Apple ID as when you bought it.
