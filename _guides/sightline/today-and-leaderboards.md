## Free trial and Sight Line Full

- **After the walkthrough:** tap **Start free trial**. 3 days of everything, free.
- **After 3 days:** Endless and the Glossy and Glowing looks need **Sight Line Full**.
- **$2.99, once.** No subscription. Yours for good.
- **Today's board is always free.** Streak and leaderboard too.
- **No ads.** Ever.
- **New phone, same Apple ID?** Tap **Restore purchase** on the card.

## Today's board

- **One board a day.** Everyone gets the same tiles.
- **One try.** Your score is your score for the day.
- **New board at 3 AM Central** (09:00 UTC).
- **Streak:** play Today on days in a row.
- **Countdown** shows when the next board comes.
- **Endless and Today** sit side by side. A game in one never touches the other.

## Leaderboards

- **Tap the trophy** to open them, right in the game.
- **Two tabs:** Today and Endless. The top 10 and your own place.
- **Endless:** your top Endless score, all time.
- **Today:** today's scores. Starts over every day at 3 AM Central, with the new board.
- **When a game ends,** you see where you stand and the top 3.
- **See all in Game Center** opens Apple's full list.
- **Not signed in to Game Center?** The game plays the same. No leaderboards.

## iCloud

- **Follows your Apple ID:** best score, Today results and streak, look, sound.
- **Another phone,** same Apple ID: it shows up there.
- **A half-played game** stays on its own phone.
- **No iCloud?** Everything still works on this phone.
