## Today's board

- **One board a day.** Everyone gets the same tiles.
- **One try.** Your score is your score for the day.
- **New board at 3 AM Central** (09:00 UTC).
- **Streak:** play Today on days in a row.
- **Countdown** shows when the next board comes.
- **Endless and Today** sit side by side. A game in one never touches the other.

## Leaderboards

- **Tap the trophy** to open them.
- **Best Score:** your top Endless score, all time.
- **Today's Board:** today's scores. Starts over with each new board.
- **When a game ends,** you see where you stand.
- **Not signed in to Game Center?** The game plays the same. No leaderboards.

## iCloud

- **Follows your Apple ID:** best score, Today results and streak, look, sound.
- **Another phone,** same Apple ID: it shows up there.
- **A half-played game** stays on its own phone.
- **No iCloud?** Everything still works on this phone.
