## The Bleach Crew

| Level | Boss | Trick |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baron Bleach | Whitewashes towers |
| 2 | Eraser Ed | Heals |
| 3 | Sir Snips | Dashes |
| 4 | Lady Lint | Makes more Blanks |
| 5 | Captain Crease | Folds into a shield |
| 6 | Smudge | Dashes, whitewashes |
| 7 | Wendy Wipe | Heals, shields |
| 8 | Count Cardboard | Shields, makes Blanks |
| 9 | Shred Head | Makes Blanks, dashes |
| 10 | Mega Baron | Whitewashes, heals, makes Blanks |

## What to do

- **Whitewashes:** tap the white tower, then **Repaint**.
- **Heals:** splat fast. Upgrade your shooters.
- **Dashes:** **Glue Gunk** slows it down.
- **Makes Blanks:** **Paint Rockets** and **Paint Bombs**.
- **Shield:** keep splatting. It opens again.

## The boss bar

- **The name and a bar** show at the top. The bar empties as you splat.
- **A boss at the castle takes 3 hearts.**
