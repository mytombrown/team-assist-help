## What it is

- **A paint tower defense game for kids.** On iPhone.
- **Baron Bleach wants Craftville colorless.** You paint it back.
- **No ads.** Progress is saved on this phone only.
- **First 10 levels of each Land are free.** See [Lands](#lands).
- **Game Center:** sign in at launch to put your high score on the board. Say no and the game plays the same.

## Start playing

- **Tap Play.**
- **The first time:** a short story plays. Tap to keep going.
- **Level 1 walks you through it.** One step at a time. Only the glowing thing works until you do it.
- **The steps:** build a Splat Shooter, start the wave, paint drops, upgrade, a Paint Rocket, the cardboard bridge, hearts.
- **Seen it before?** Level 1 plays on its own. Tap **Show me again** on the title screen to see it again (with a **Skip** button).

## How a level plays

- **Blanks walk the path** to your castle.
- **Build towers** beside the path. They splat the Blanks back to color.
- **Each pop gives paint drops.** Spend them on more towers.
- **Tap Start wave** when you are ready. **1x / 2x** speeds it up.
- **Every level is a bit harder** than the one before.
- **A boss comes on every 10th level**, in the last wave.

## Hearts and stars

- **You have 5 hearts.** A Blank at the castle takes 1. A boss takes 3.
- **5 hearts left:** 3 stars. **3–4:** 2 stars. **1–2:** 1 star.
- **0 hearts:** "Oh no, bleached!" Tap **Try again**.
- **3 stars on Hard only.** Easy tops out at 2.

## Easy or Hard

- **Pick on the map.** Tap Easy or Hard.
- **Hard:** more Blanks, faster, tougher. Bosses are stronger.
- **Hard starts small:** the same paint as Easy. Earn more and build out.
- **10 waves on Hard,** light at first, heavy at the end.
- **Hard scores x2.**

## Back spots

- **From level 21,** each board has 2 spots far from the path.
- **Paint Bomb only** on those spots. Its long reach covers the path.

## Score

- **Your score has 3 parts.** The results card shows each one.
- **Time bonus:** 5 points a second you beat the clock (wins only).
- **Drops earned:** 1 point for each paint drop you earn.
- **Hearts left:** 100 points a heart (wins only).
- **Total score:** your best on each level, added up. It shows on the map and goes to Game Center if you are signed in.
- **Trophy button** on the map and results screen opens the Game Center board (signed in only).

## Lands

- **Every 100 levels is a Land.** Tap Play, then tap a Land.
- **Land 1, Paint Park:** levels 1-100, Baron Bleach.
- **Land 2, Spin Cycle:** levels 101-200. Count Socksworth, a colorless sock, wants all the laundry colorless too.
- **Land 2 opens** once you clear level 10 of Land 1. No purchase needed.
- **Each Land keeps its own progress.**
- **Levels 1-10 of each Land are free.**
- **$0.99 opens the rest** of that Land (levels 11-100). One purchase per Land.
- **A grown-up check** comes before every buy button.
- **Restore Purchases** is on the Lands screen. Use it on a new phone.

## Land 2 Blanks

- **Lint Bunnies:** small and fast, in swarms.
- **Dryer Sheets:** float over the path. Bombs and glue miss them; splats pop them.
- **Hanger Knights:** armored.
- **Bleach Bubbles:** split in 2 when popped.
- **Lost Socks:** vanish, then pop up further on.
- **Steam Iron:** slow and heavy. Takes 2 hearts.
- **New tower at level 111: Static Cling Zapper.** Shocks a group and freezes it for 5 seconds. Land 2 and later only.

## The map

- **100 levels in each Land**, 10 places of 10 levels.
- **Each place has its own colors** and a boss on its 10th level.
- **The story** plays at level 1 and around each boss.
- **Win a level** to open the next one.
- **Your best stars** show on each level.
- **Easy / Hard** sits under the top bar, never over your score.

## iPhone Duo

- **Open and held sideways:** the board stays the same size in the middle.
  - **Left side:** home, wave, paint drops, paper and hearts.
  - **Right side:** Start wave and speed. Tap a spot and the towers show here.
- **Hearts, Help and the level button stay clear of the camera.**
- **Every other iPhone** plays upright, the same as before.
