## Build a tower

- **Tap a spot** beside the path.
- **Pick a tower.** The number is its cost in paint drops.
- **Tap a tower to place it.**

## The four towers

- **You start with two.** More unlock as you go. Pip shows each new one the first time.

| Tower | Cost | Unlocks | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Splat Shooter | 50 | Level 1 | Fast paintballs |
| Paint Rocket | 90 | Level 1 | Splash on a group |
| Glue Gunk | 60 | Level 11 | Sticky. Slows Blanks |
| Paint Bomb | 110 | Level 21 | Colors a whole area |

## Upgrade

- **Tap your tower**, then **Upgrade**. Each tower upgrades twice.
- **Splat Shooter** → Double → Rainbow
- **Paint Rocket** → Big Splash → Triple
- **Paint Bomb** → Puddle → Cluster
- **Glue Gunk** → Wide Glue → Freeze
- **Max level!** means it is done.

## Sell

- **Tap your tower**, then **Sell**. You get some drops back.

## A white tower

- **A boss whitewashed it.** It stops working.
- **Tap it**, then **Repaint**.

## Tips

- **Glue + Splat Shooter** is a good pair.
- **Rockets and bombs** help when Blanks bunch up.
