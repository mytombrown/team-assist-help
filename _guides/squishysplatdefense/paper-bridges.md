## What it does

- **Each map has a torn-paper gap.**
- **Fold a bridge** and the Blanks take the **longer path**.
- **Longer path = more time to splat.**

## How to fold one

- **Earn craft paper.** Level 1: clear wave 2. Other levels: clear wave 1.
- **Tap the gap.**
- **Tap Fold it.** It costs 1 craft paper.
- **"Needs craft paper"** means you have not earned it yet.

## Tip

- **Put towers along the new path** after you fold the bridge.
