## What it is

- **A money game** for kids 4 to 12.
- **US coins and bills:** penny, nickel, dime, quarter, $1, $5.
- **iPhone and iPad.**
- **No ads. Nothing online. No accounts.**

## First launch

- **Pick a player** on the first screen, or make one.
- **The age sets where you start.** Older kids skip the easy shifts.

| Age | Shown under the age | Starts at |
|---|---|---|
| Under 6 | **Coins and counting** | Level 1 |
| 6–7 | **Enough and change** | Level 2 |
| 8–9 | **Dollars and cents** | Level 4 |
| 10+ | **Rush hour** | Level 6 |

## The game screen

- **Top left: the X.** Stops the game. Points so far are saved.
- **Level.** Your level now.
- **Score**, with **Customer 1 of 5** under it.
- **Coins.** Your coins.
- **Hammer button.** Opens **Upgrade the stand**.
- **The stand.** Your squishy, the customer, and the tip jar.
- **The order card.** What the customer asks.
