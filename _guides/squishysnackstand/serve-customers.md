## The levels

| Level | What it asks |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Which coin is it?** |
| 2 | **Count the coins, one kind at a time** |
| 3 | **Count mixed coins** |
| 4 | **Enough, or not enough?** |
| 5 | **Give the right change** |
| 6 | **Pay the supplier exactly — bills too** |
| 7 | **Dollars and cents** |
| 8+ | **Rush hour! Every kind of customer, on the clock** |

## Tap a choice

- **Name the coin:** one coin shows at its real size. Tap its name.
- **Count:** see **They gave you:**. Tap the total.
- **Enough:** tap **Yes, enough** or **No, not enough**.

## Use the tray

- **Tap coins in the Till.** Each one goes on the tray.
- **The tray total shows** at the top right of the tray.
- **Tap a coin on the tray** to take it off.
- **Undo** takes off the last coin.
- **Making change:** tap **Give change**.
- **Paying the supplier:** the tray says **Your purse pays:**. Tap **Pay**.

## Right, wrong, missed

- **Right first try:** **Yay! Thank you!** 5 × your level in points, and a tip in the jar.
- **Right on the second try:** **Yay! Got it!** Same points, no tip.
- **First miss:** **Hmm, not quite.** You see a hint. Tap **Try again**.
- **Second miss:** **Oh no, they left.** You see the answer. No points.
- **Tap Next customer** to go on.

## Rush hour (level 8 and up)

- **A line of customers waits.**
- **A timer bar shows on each customer.** It turns red at 5 seconds.
- **24 seconds a customer on level 8.** 2 fewer each level after. Never under 10.
- **Time runs out = they leave.** No points.

## End of the shift

- **After five customers:** **Shift … done!**
- **Bonus:** 25 × your level.
- **Perfect shift!** with stars if every customer was right first try.
- **Coins and new badges** show here as **Achievement: …**.
- **Next:** what the next level is about.
- **Next shift** goes up one level.
- **Stop for now** ends the game.
