## I can't tap Give change or Pay

- **The tray is empty.** Tap at least one coin in the **Till** first.
- **A message is up.** Tap its button first.

## My change was marked wrong

- **The tray total must match exactly.**
- **Check the total** on the tray before you tap.
- **Tap a coin on the tray** to take it off. Or tap **Undo**.

## I can't buy an upgrade

- **Not enough coins.** The price button is grey.
- **Already bought.** It says **Owned**.

## The timer is too fast

- **The timer starts at level 8.** It gets shorter each level.
- **It never goes under 10 seconds.**

## It's too hard or too easy

- **The start level comes from the player's age.**
- **Each finished shift moves up one level.**

## Did I lose my points?

- **Points are saved** when a shift ends, when you tap **Stop for now**, or when you tap the X.
