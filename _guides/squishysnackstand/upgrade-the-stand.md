## Open it

- **Tap the hammer button** at the top of the game screen.
- **Upgrade the stand** opens.
- **Your coins show** at the top.

## The upgrades

| Upgrade | Coins |
|---|---|
| **Big umbrella** | 300 |
| **Painted sign** | 600 |
| **String lights** | 900 |
| **Second window** | 1500 |

## Buy one

- **Tap the coin price** next to an upgrade.
- **Bought ones say Owned.**
- **Each one is drawn on your stand.**
- **Tap Done** to go back.

## Coins

- **You earn coins by playing.**
- **Coins earned show** at the end of each shift.

## Badges

- **Seven ladders:** **Customers served**, **Perfect shifts**, **In a row**, **Change made exactly**, **Rush hours survived**, **All-time points**, **The stand**.
- **The stand ladder:** buy one upgrade, then buy them all.
