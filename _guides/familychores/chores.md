## Make a chore (parents)

- **Tap New Chore** on **Home** or the **Chores** tab.
- **Title:** what the chore is. Add **Instructions** if you want.
- **Points:** set how much it's **Worth**.
- **Assigned to:** turn on each child who does it.
- **More than one child?** Pick **Each child does it** or **Take turns**.
- **Tap Create.**

## Schedule options

- **Repeats:** **One time**, **Daily**, **Certain days**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Every N days**.
- **Has a due time:** turn on to set **Due by**.
- **If it's missed:** **Skip it**, **Stay available**, or **Roll to next day**.

## How it's checked off

- **Requires:** **Just check it off**, **Check off + note**, or **Enter an amount** (like minutes read).
- **Parent approval required:** on by default. Points wait for a parent.

## Mark a chore done

- **Child:** on **Home**, find it under **Today**, tap **Mark Done**, then **Done**.
- **Parent for a child:** on the **Chores** tab under **Today**, tap **Mark Done**.
- **Waiting on a parent** shows until it's approved.

## Approve or decline (parents)

- **On Home, see Chore approvals.**
- **Tap Approve** to give the points.
- **Tap Decline,** type a **Reason**, tap **Submit**.
- **Made a mistake?** On the **Chores** tab, tap **Undo** on an approved chore and give a reason.

## Change or remove a chore

- **Chores tab > All Chores.** Tap **Edit** or **Remove**.
- **Remove** stops it from now on. Points already earned stay.
