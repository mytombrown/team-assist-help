Chores is chores, points, and rewards for the whole family. Kids do chores, earn points, and spend them on rewards you set up.

## First launch

- **Setting up the family?** Tap **Create a Family**.
- **Someone invited you?** Tap **Join a Family**.
- **Opened an invite link?** Tap **Join Now** on the welcome screen.

## Create a family

- **Type a Family name** (like "The Browns").
- **Type Your name** and pick a color.
- **Tap Create.**
- **You become the Owner** of the family.

## The four tabs

- **Home:** parents see the dashboard. Kids see their points and today's chores.
- **Chores:** today's chores and the full chore list.
- **Rewards:** what points can buy.
- **Activity:** every time points were earned, spent or changed.

## Settings

- **Tap the gear** at the top of **Home**.
- **Shows** the family name, members, and **Sync Status**.
