## Add someone with no phone

- **On Home, tap Add Family Member.** Only the **Owner** sees it.
- **Type their Name.** Pick **Child** or **Parent**, and a color.
- **Tap Add.**
- **No device or Apple ID needed.**
- **You can mark chores done and redeem rewards for them.**

## Invite someone with their own device

- **On Home, tap Invite Family Member.** Or in **Settings**, tap **Invite With a Code**.
- **The Invite screen** shows a QR code and a 6-character code.
- **Share it:** read them the code, let them scan the QR code, or tap **Share Link**.
- **Need a fresh one?** Tap **New Code**.

## Join on the other device

- **Open Chores and tap Join a Family.**
- **Type the 6-character code** and tap **Continue**, or tap **Scan QR Code**.
- **Already added by a parent?** Under **Is one of these you?**, tap your name. Your points and history come with you.
- **Otherwise:** type **Your name**, pick **Parent** or **Child**, tap **Create Profile**.

## Remove someone

- **Settings > Members,** tap **Remove** next to them. Owner only.
- **Or on Home,** press and hold a child, then tap **Remove** with their name.
- **Their past points and chore history** stay in **Activity**.
