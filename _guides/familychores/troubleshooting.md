## Orange iCloud banner at the top

- **"Sign in to iCloud":** sign in to iCloud in iPhone **Settings**. Your data stays on this phone meanwhile.
- **"Can't reach iCloud right now":** you're offline. It syncs when you're back online.
- **Other iCloud messages:** your data is still saved on this phone. Email support with the message.
- **Tap the x** to hide the banner.

## Changes not showing on another phone

- **Pull down** on any tab to refresh.
- **Settings > Sync Status,** tap **Sync Now and Refresh**.
- **Still stuck?** Compare **Sync Status** on both phones. The zone owner and family ID must match.

## Can't join with a code

- **Check the 6-character code** and try again.
- **Codes can expire.** Ask for a new one. They tap **New Code** on the Invite screen.
- **No Scan QR Code button:** this phone can't scan. Type the code instead.
- **Camera blocked:** iPhone **Settings** > **Apps** > **Chores**, allow Camera.

## Can't find a button

- **Add Family Member, Invite, Remove member:** **Owner** only.
- **New Chore, New Reward, Adjust, Approve:** parents only.
- **Redeem greyed out:** not enough available points yet.

## "Something went wrong"

- **"You don't have permission to do that":** ask a parent or the Owner.
- **Otherwise:** try again. Then email support.

## Start over

- **Settings > Start Over > Reset This Device:** signs this phone out. The family stays in iCloud.
- **Delete All Family Data:** Owner only. Erases everything for everyone. Can't be undone.

## Contact support

- **Email [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).**
- **Include** what you tapped and any message you saw.
