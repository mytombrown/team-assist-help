## Make a reward (parents)

- **Tap New Reward** on **Home** or the **Rewards** tab.
- **Title:** what they get. Add a **Description** if you want.
- **Cost:** how many points it takes.
- **Icon:** tap one.
- **Parent approval required:** on by default.
- **Limited quantity:** turn on to cap how many are available.
- **Only certain children:** turn on to show it to some kids only.
- **Tap Create.**

## Redeem a reward

- **Open the Rewards tab.**
- **Parent?** Pick the child at the top first.
- **Tap Redeem,** then **Confirm**.
- **Redeem is greyed out** until there are enough points.
- **Needing approval?** It shows under **My requests** (or the child's requests) until a parent decides. Tap **Cancel** to take it back.

## Approve a reward (parents)

- **On Home, see Reward requests.**
- **Tap Approve,** or **Decline** with a reason.

## Save up for a goal

- **Tap Set Goal** on a reward.
- **The goal and progress** show on the child's **Home**.

## Points vs available

- **Available** is what can be spent now.
- **Points held for a waiting reward request** are not available.

## Add or take away points (parents)

- **On Home, tap Adjust** next to a child.
- **Type an amount:** like 10, or -10 to take away.
- **Type a reason** and tap **Save**.

## See the history

- **Activity tab:** every point earned, spent, held, reversed or adjusted.
- **Kids see only their own.**
