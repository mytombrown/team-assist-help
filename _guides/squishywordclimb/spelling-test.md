## Make a list

- **Tap Spelling Test**, then **New list**.
- **List name:** it starts as "Week of" and today's date. Change it if you like.
- **Type or paste the words** under **Words, one per line**.
- **Or read them off the sheet:**
  - **Take a picture** uses the camera.
  - **Photo** picks a photo you already have.
- **Check the words.** The count and the words show under the box. Fix any mistakes.
- **Tap Save list.**

## Take the test

- **Tap the list** under **Spelling lists**.
- **Tap Listen.** The phone says the word three times, nice and slow.
- **Hear it again** repeats it.
- **Tap or swipe the letters** to spell it. **Undo** takes one back.
- **Tap Check.**

## Points

- **First try:** 10 points.
- **Second try:** 5 points.
- **A miss:** **Not quite**. The word shows, then comes back later in the test.
- **After 3 tries** it counts as missed.

## The results

- **Results show** **First try**, **Second try** and **To practice**.
- **Missed words come first** the next time this player takes that list.
- **The list shows the last score** under its name.

## Change or delete a list

- **Tap the pencil** on a list to edit it.
- **Delete list**, then **Yes, delete**. The words go away. Scores stay with the player.
