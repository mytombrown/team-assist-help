## How a word plays

- **You see:** the first letter, an emoji clue, and scrambled letter tiles.
- **Tap the letters, or swipe through them.**
- **Undo** takes back a letter.
- **Faster is more points.** As the clock runs, more letters fill in and the clue gets easier. The points drop.
- **The clock:** 15 seconds for ages 4–5. 10 seconds for everyone else.
- **Time's up!** shows the word.

## Skip

- **Skip** moves on. It costs 5 points.

## The ladder

- **Each word is one rung.** **Rung 1 of 5** shows where you are.
- **Top of the ladder!** after 5 words. Tap **Level up!**
- **Level … done!** shows the bonus and coins.
- **Next Level** brings longer words and fewer free letters.
- **Stop for now** ends the game.

## Stop early

- **Tap Stop**, then confirm **Stop climbing?**
