## What it is

- **A word game for kids 4 to 12.** On iPhone and iPad.
- **Two ways to play:** **Word Climb** and **Spelling Test**.
- **No ads. Nothing online.** Players are saved on this device only.

## Make a player

- **Tap New player.**
- **Type a name** under **What's your name?**
- **Pick an age** under **How old are you?**
- **Pick a squishy** under **Your squishy**, then tap **Let's go!**

## What the age does

| Age | Words |
|---|---|
| 4–5 | Short words, picture clues |
| 6–7 | Everyday words |
| 8–9 | Bigger words |
| 10–12 | Big words, real definitions |

## Start playing

- **Tap a player.** One player at a time.
- **Tap Play.**
- **Pick a game:** **Word Climb** or **Spelling Test**.
- **Players** (top left) goes back.

## Coins and the shop

- **Games earn coins.** Each player has their own.
- **Tap Shop & stats** on a player to spend them on **Squishies** and **Colors**.
- **Achievements** on the same page show badges and the **Next badge**.
- **Delete player** removes the player, coins and badges.
