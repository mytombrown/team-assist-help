## "No words found"

- **Try a straighter, brighter photo.** Fill the screen with the list.
- **Or type the words** under **Words, one per line**.

## "Could not read that photo"

- **Pick another photo**, or take a new one.

## Take a picture is grey

- **This device has no camera.** Use **Photo** or type the words.

## The camera won't open

- **Allow the camera:** Settings › Squishy Words › Camera.
- **The photo stays on this phone.**

## The voice is hard to understand

- **The app shows a card:** "Hard to understand? A clearer voice is a free download."
- **Tap Open Settings.**
- **Go to** Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English › Ava (Enhanced).
- **Download it.** The app uses it next time.

## No sound

- **Check the speaker button** on the player screen.
- **Check the silent switch** and the volume.

## Players or coins are gone

- **Players are saved on this device only.** They do not move to a new device.
- **Deleting the app deletes the players and lists.**

## Contact support

- **Email** [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).
- **Say** your device, and what you tapped.
