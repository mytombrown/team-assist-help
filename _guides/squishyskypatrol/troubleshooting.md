## No sound

- **Check the silent switch** and the volume. The game follows the silent switch.

## A level is too hard

- **Grab the gold cloud's shield.** It keeps you safe for a few seconds.
- **Keep moving.** Rumble clouds drop raindrops straight down.
- **Lost?** Tap **Try again**. Hearts reset.

## The next level is locked

- **Win the level before it.** Any win opens the next one.

## My score is not on Game Center

- **Sign in:** Settings › Game Center. Then open the game again.
- **Finish a level.** The high score is sent after each level.
- **No High Scores button?** You are not signed in to Game Center.

## Stars or levels are gone

- **Progress is saved on this phone only.** It does not move to a new phone.
- **Deleting the app deletes the progress.**

## Contact support

- **Email** [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).
- **Say** your phone, the level, and what you tapped.
