## What it is

- **A paper-plane game for kids.** On iPhone.
- **Big Bubble Bob made the clouds grumpy.** You pop them happy again.
- **No ads.**
- **Progress follows you.** Stars, levels and score save to your own iCloud. Another iPhone on the same Apple ID picks up where you left off.
  - **Purchases** come back with Restore Purchases, not iCloud.
  - **iCloud off** for the app: progress stays on this phone only.
- **Meet the Cast:** tap it on the title screen. Heroes and Villains, each with a picture and a short bio.
- **Game Center:** sign in at launch to put your high score on the board. Say no and the game plays the same.
- **First 10 levels of each Land are free.** See [Lands](#lands).

## Lands

- **Every 100 levels is a Land.** Tap Play, then tap a Land.
- **Land 1, Rainbow Skies:** levels 1-100.
- **Land 2, Cosmic Coast:** levels 101-200, in space. Comet puffs zoom, asteroid clouds break apart, UFO puffs zig-zag, black-hole clouds pull your bubbles off course.
- **Land 2 opens** once you clear level 10 of Land 1. No purchase needed.
- **Each Land keeps its own progress.**
- **Levels 1-10 of each Land are free.** **$0.99 opens the rest** of that Land (11-100). One purchase per Land.
- **A grown-up check** comes before every buy button.
- **Restore Purchases** is on the Lands screen. Use it on a new phone.
- **Each Land has its own ship.** Rainbow Skies: the paper plane. Cosmic Coast: a rocket jet that flies faster, from its very first level.
- **Cosmic Coast sidekick:** from level 111 (after the $0.99), a sidekick clips on and fires a second stream — until a cloud hits you.

## Start playing

- **Tap Play.**
- **The first time:** a short story plays. Tap to keep going.
- **Drag with one thumb.** The plane fires bubbles by itself. Each bubble has a dark ring, so you can see it on every sky.
- **Fly anywhere.** The plane reaches all the way up, just under the score and hearts. Bumping a boss still costs a heart.

## The clouds

- **Puff:** 1 hit. **Drizzle:** 2 hits, zigzags. **Rumble:** 4 hits, drops raindrops.
- **Gold cloud:** drops a shield. It keeps you safe for a few seconds.
- **A boss** waits at the end of every world: levels 10, 20, 30 … 200. In space: the Gloom Moon and Meteor Mike.

## Power-ups

- **Fast Shot (from level 11):** catch the pink bubble. You fire twice as fast.
  - **A hit takes it away.** It also ends with the level.
- **Sun Blast (from level 21):** tap the sun. Every cloud on screen pops.
  - **Once a level.** The sun goes grey after. The boss is not popped.

## Hearts and stars

- **You have 3 hearts.** A raindrop or a cloud bump takes 1.
- **1 star:** win the level. **2 stars:** pop half the clouds. **3 stars:** pop most of them with 2 hearts left.
  - **3 stars on Hard only.** Easy tops out at 2.

## Easy or Hard

- **Pick on the map.** Tap Easy or Hard.
- **Hard:** more clouds, faster, tougher. Bosses are stronger.
- **Hard scores x2.**

## Score

- **Level score = time + coins + hearts.**
  - **Time:** 5 for each second under par (wins only).
  - **Coins:** 10 for each jelly coin.
  - **Hearts:** 100 for each heart left (wins only).
- **The results screen** shows all 3 parts.
- **Total score** = each level's best, added up. It shows on the map.
- **Signed in to Game Center?** Your total goes on the High Score board. Tap **High Scores** on the title screen, or the **trophy** on the map or results screen, to see it.

## The map

- **100 levels in 10 worlds in each Land.** Puffy Peaks first, then Thunder Town and more.
- **Win a level** to open the next one.
- **Your best stars** show on each level.
- **The story** plays before level 1 and on every 10th level.

## iPhone Duo

- **Open and held sideways:** the game stays the same size in the middle.
  - **Left side:** score, coins, level and world.
  - **Right side:** hearts, Fast Shot and the sun.
- **Hearts stay clear of the camera** on every screen.
- **Every other iPhone** plays upright, the same as before.
