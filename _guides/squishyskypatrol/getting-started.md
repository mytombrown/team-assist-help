## What it is

- **Pip's Arcade is DotVille,** a little paper town. On iPhone.
- **Every house is something to do.** Tap a house to go in.
- **No ads.**
- **This app used to be Squishy Sky Patrol.** Your Sky Patrol levels, stars and purchases are still here.

## The town

- **Sky Patrol:** fly a paper plane and pop grumpy clouds. See [Sky Patrol]({{ '/squishyskypatrol/sky-patrol/' | relative_url }}).
- **Splat Defense:** build paint towers and splat the Blanks back into color. See [Splat Defense help]({{ '/squishysplatdefense/' | relative_url }}).
- **Claw Machine:** grab a dot for your room. See [Claw Machine]({{ '/squishyskypatrol/claw-machine/' | relative_url }}).
- **Prize Counter:** trade tickets for prizes. See [Tickets and prizes]({{ '/squishyskypatrol/tickets-and-prizes/' | relative_url }}).
- **All games** in a game takes you back to the town.

## The badges, top left

- **Stars:** your stars from every game, added up. Tap it to open **Pip's closet.**
- **Tickets:** tap it to open the Prize Counter.
- **My Room:** tap it to open your room. See [My Room]({{ '/squishyskypatrol/my-room/' | relative_url }}).

## Pip's closet

- **Stars open hats for Pip:** Party Hat 5, Flower Crown 15, Big Bow 30, Gold Crown 50, Wizard Hat 80.
- **Or buy a hat** at the Prize Counter with tickets.
- **Pip wears it in every game.**

## Daily challenge

- **Each day, one game is picked.** Earn 2 new stars in it.
- **Done = 3 bonus stars** for Pip's closet.

## Saving

- **Each game keeps its own lands, levels and stars,** saved to your own iCloud.
- **Tickets, prizes, your room and Pip's outfit** are kept on this phone.
- **Purchases** come back with Restore Purchases, in each game's Lands screen.
