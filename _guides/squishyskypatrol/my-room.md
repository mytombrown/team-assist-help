## Open it

- **In the town:** tap **My Room,** top left.
- **In the Claw Machine:** tap My Room.

## What is in it

- **Every dot you win** at the Claw Machine or buy at the Prize Counter.
- **Room decor** you buy.
- **Hats** you buy. They start on the windowsill.
- **DotVille Quest friends** you buy.
- **Top right** says how many of the 10 dots you have.
- **Empty room?** Win a dot in the Claw Machine and it comes here.

## Move things

- **Drag anything** where you like.
- **It stays there,** even after you close the app.

## Who is that?

- **Tap a dot** or a Quest friend. A card tells you who they are.
- **Tap Close** to go back.
