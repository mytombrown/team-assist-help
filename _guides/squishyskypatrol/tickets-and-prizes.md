## Earn tickets

- **Every mission pays** by its best stars: **1 star = 1, 2 stars = 2, 3 stars = 5.**
- **Missions** are Sky Patrol levels, Splat Defense levels and the Claw Machine lesson.
- **Beat your best:** you get the difference. 1 star to 3 stars pays 4 more.
- **Claw Machine:** extra wins after the day's Dot pay 1 each.
- **The town says** "+ tickets!" when you come back with new ones.
- **Tickets can never be bought.** There is no money in tickets.

## The Prize Counter

- **Tap the Prize Counter house,** or the tickets badge in the town.
- **Pip is behind the counter.** Tap Pip to read Pip's story.
- **Tap a prize** to get it. Pip asks first: tap **Yes!**
- **Not enough tickets?** Pip says how many more you need.

## The prizes

- **Dots for your room:** 8 each. Buy the same Dot again and it grows, up to large.
- **Hats for Pip:** Party Hat 4, Flower Crown 6, Big Bow 10, Gold Crown 15, Wizard Hat 23. Pip can wear it, and it stands in your room.
- **Room decor:** flowers, mushrooms, lollipops and bushes, 3 to 6.
- **DotVille Quest friends:** Victoria, Sunny, Nika, Pengu, Owl, Axo, Dragon, Piggy, Uni and Pell, 10 to 21.
- **Dots and Quest friends** open a card with who they are. Tap **Get** on the card to buy.
- **Got it** means you have it. Hats, decor and friends are once each.
