## Search is greyed out

- **No locations:** click **Add Location** first.
- **Search Name:** type something in the **Name** box.
- **Custom…:** type at least one file ending.
- **Already searching:** wait, or click **Stop**.

## "Nothing found"

- **Try a different filter** or location.
- **Check the Size filter.** Set it to **Any size**.
- **Check Res** for videos. Set it to **Any**.
- **Hidden files are skipped.**
- **Files inside packages are skipped** — for example, a Photos library.

## A location says "Not connected — skipped"

- **The drive isn't connected** right now.
- **Connect it,** then search again.
- **Or remove it:** point at it and click the **x**.

## A "Heads up" message

- **"…duplicate set(s) have every copy selected."** Uncheck one copy in each group. Then try again.
- **"…file(s) failed"** — some files couldn't be moved or deleted. The box lists them and why.

## My results are gone

- **Results are not kept.** Quitting or closing the window erases them.
- **Search again** to get them back.

## Can I get deleted files back?

- **No.** **Delete…** removes files for good. They do not go to the Trash.
