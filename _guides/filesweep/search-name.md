## Run the search

1. **Click Search Name** at the top.
2. **Type part of the name** in the **Name** box.
3. **Press Return**, or click **Search**.

## How it matches

- **Anywhere in the name.** `tom` finds `tom.jpg` and `Photos of Tom.pdf`.
- **Capitals don't matter.** `tom` finds `TOM`, `Tom` and `tom`.
- **Accents don't matter.** `e` matches `é`.
- **Size filter** works here too.

## Results

- **Sorted by name.**
- **Right-click a row** and choose **Show in Finder** to see it.
