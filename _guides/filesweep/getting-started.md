## What it does

- **Finds duplicate files** — the same file saved more than once.
- **Finds files of one type** — like all videos or all images.
- **Finds files by name.**
- **Moves or deletes** the files you check.
- **Runs on a Mac** (macOS 14 or later).

## The window

- **Left side:** **LOCATIONS** — the folders and drives it searches.
- **Top:** three modes — **Find Duplicates**, **Find File Type** and **Search Name**.
- **Below that:** filters and the **Search** button.
- **Middle:** your results.
- **Bottom:** **Select**, **Move…** and **Delete…**.

## Add a location

- **Click Add Location**, pick one or more folders or network drives, then click **Add**.
- **Or drag** folders onto the **LOCATIONS** list.
- **To remove one,** point at it and click the **x**.

## Your first search

1. **Add a location.**
2. **Pick a mode** at the top.
3. **Click Search**, or press **⌘↩** (Command-Return).

## What it keeps

- **Only your locations.** The list is there next time.
- **Results are not kept.** Quitting erases them.
- **Closing the window quits the app.**
