## Check files

- **Click a row** to check or uncheck it.
- **Select › All** checks every result.
- **Select › None** unchecks everything.
- **The bottom left** shows how many are checked and their total size.

## Move files

1. **Click Move…**
2. **Pick a folder.** Or click **New Folder** to make one.
3. **Click Move Here.**

- **Name already used:** the moved file gets a number, like `photo 2.jpg`.
- **Already in that folder:** the file is left where it is.

## Delete files

1. **Click Delete…**
2. **Read the box.** It shows how many files and how much space.
3. **Click Delete** to go ahead, or **Cancel**.

- **Delete is permanent.** Files do **not** go to the Trash.
- **It can't be undone.** If you're not sure, use **Move…** instead.

## Duplicates

- **Leave one copy** in every group unchecked.
- **If every copy is checked,** the app stops and asks you to uncheck one.

## When it's done

- **Moved or deleted files** leave the results.
- **The status line** says how many worked, and how many failed.
