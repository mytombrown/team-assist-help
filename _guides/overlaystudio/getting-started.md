## Two modes

The switch at the top of the window picks one.

- **Create:** make and change templates. Nothing here reaches the air.
- **Play:** put templates on air with your words in them.

## Your first graphic

1. **In Create,** click **+ New template**.
2. **Add** something with **+ Text**, **+ Shape** or **+ Image**.
3. **Switch to Play** and click **Add from template**.
4. **Double-click the new button,** type your words, click **Save**.
5. **Click the button,** then press **Space** to take it to air.

## See it in your show

- **Click Feed links** in Play.
- **Paste the Program link** into OBS or vMix.
