## The feed is blank

- **Blank is normal** when nothing is on air. Press **Take** in Play.
- **Check the app is open** and the Mac is awake.
- **On another computer,** use the **Your network** link, not **This Mac**.

## I changed a template and air didn't change

That's on purpose. Take the graphic again to air your changes.

## "No link after 30 seconds"

- **Check the Mac's internet connection,** then turn the switch off and on.
- **A strict firewall** can block the connection. Try another network.

## The Outside link stopped

- **Did the switch go off,** or the app close? Turn it on again. You'll get a new link.
- **Did someone click New Key?** Copy the new link.

## A Photoshop file looks different

Layer effects, blend modes and clipping don't come in. Add effects again in Create.
