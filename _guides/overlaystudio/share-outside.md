## Turn it on

1. **In Play,** click **Feed links**.
2. **Turn on Share outside my network.** Wait for **Live**.
3. **Copy the Outside link,** or click the QR code button.

No account and no router setup.

## Good to know

- **The link changes** each time you turn the switch on.
- **New Key** makes a new link. The old one stops right away.
- **Treat the link like a password.** Anyone who has it can see what's on air.
- **Your graphics pass through Cloudflare** while the switch is on.

## Turn it off

Turn the switch off. The Outside link stops working.
