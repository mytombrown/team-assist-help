## Send an idea

- **Click Request a feature** at the top of the window. Or use **Help > Request a Feature**.
- **Type your idea.** That's the only box you have to fill in.
- **Why it helps** and **Email for a reply** are optional.
- **Click Send.** Your mail app opens with it filled in. Send it from there.

## No mail app?

Click **Copy**. Paste it into any email to [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).

## What gets sent

Only what you typed, the app version and your macOS version. Nothing is sent until you send the email.
