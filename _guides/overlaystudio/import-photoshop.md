## Import it

1. **In Create,** click **Import Photoshop…**, or press ⇧⌘I.
2. **Pick a .psd or .psb file.**
3. **A new template opens** with your layers in it.

A short summary tells you what came in, and anything it couldn't bring.

## What comes in

- **Picture layers,** with their transparency and masks.
- **Text layers,** as text you can change.
- **Groups** are flattened into separate layers.

## What doesn't

- **Layer effects** such as shadows and strokes. Add them again in Overlay Studio.
- **Blend modes and clipping.**
