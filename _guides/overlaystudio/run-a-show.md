## The screen

- **Preview, on the left:** what's next. Clicking a button puts it here.
- **Program, on the right:** what's on air.
- **Below them:** your buttons.

## Add a button

- **Click Add from template** and pick one. Add as many as you need.
- **Each button is named** after its first line of text.

## Change the words

1. **Double-click the button.**
2. **Type your lines.** Misspelled words get flagged and fixed as you type.
3. **Click Save.**

## Put it on air

- **Take, or Space:** puts what's in Preview on air.
- **Out:** takes the live button off air.
- **Clear all:** takes everything off air.

## More

- **Right-click a button** for Edit Words…, All Settings…, Duplicate, Move Earlier, Move Later and Delete.
- **Options** has auto-advance after a hold time, and loop back to the first button.
