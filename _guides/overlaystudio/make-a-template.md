## Start one

- **Click + New template.** Choose **Blank, 16:9** or **Blank, 9:16** for vertical video.
- **Rename it:** click the name above the canvas, type, press Return.

## Add to it

- **+ Text:** words you can change later in Play.
- **+ Shape:** a bar or panel behind your words.
- **+ Image:** a PNG, JPEG or GIF. PNG transparency stays transparent on air.

## Style it

- **Click an item** on the canvas to open its settings.
- **Text and shapes** can have a drop shadow, outline, glow or gradient.
- **Drag** to move an item.
- **Layers** lists everything, and sets what sits on top.

## Transitions

- **Click In and Out** to set how this template arrives and leaves.
- **Try In and Try Out** play it on the canvas only. Nothing goes on air.

## Fix a mistake

- **Undo:** ⌘Z. **Redo:** ⇧⌘Z.
- **Cut, Copy, Paste and Duplicate** are in the Edit menu.

## Does this change what's on air?

No. A graphic on air keeps what it had when you took it, until you take it again.
