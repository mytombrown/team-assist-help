## Send

1. **Step 1:** tap **Caller**.
2. **Step 2:** choose **Send my camera to them**.
3. **Host** and **Port:** the receiver's address.
4. **Next** through the steps, then **Connect**.
5. **Allow** the camera and microphone when asked.

## Video settings

These are in **Show all settings**, under **Video**.

- **Resolution:** 480p, 720p or 1080p.
- **Orientation:** Landscape or Portrait. iPhone and iPad only.
- **Bitrate:** 1 to 12 Mb/s.
- **The Mac** always sends the camera as it delivers it, landscape.

## Good to know

- **iPhone and iPad** use the rear camera and the default microphone.
- **One caller at a time:** many receivers only accept one. Disconnect any other sender first.
- **Watch the numbers:** see [what the numbers mean]({{ '/srtstreamtester/read-the-numbers/' | relative_url }}).
