## Connect

1. **Step 1:** tap **Caller**.
2. **Step 2:** choose **Watch their stream**.
3. **Host:** the IP or name you were given.
4. **Port:** the port you were given.
5. **Next** through the steps, then **Connect**.

## Paste a whole link

- **Got an srt:// link?** Paste it into the box on step 2.
- **The fields fill in** and it says **Filled in from the link.**
- **Will dial:** shows the exact link the app will use.

## Latency, passphrase, stream ID

- **Latency:** how long the app waits so lost packets can be resent. 120 ms suits a local network. Use more over the internet.
- **Latency is in milliseconds.** Some links quote microseconds — `latency=5000000` means type `5000`.
- **Passphrase:** must match the other end. 10 to 79 characters. Leave empty if they have none.
- **Stream ID:** only if you were given one.

## Save a connection

- **Save…** names the connection. It keeps the address, port, latency, passphrase and stream ID.
- **Load saved** brings one back.
- **Same name** replaces the old one.
- **Recent** (in **Show all settings**) lists your last connections. Swipe to delete one.

## While it plays

- **Speaker button and slider:** mute and volume.
- **Audio:** pick the audio track, or **Off**.
- **Video**, **SCTE-35** and **CC** turn the picture, ad-cue card and captions on or off.
- **Stop** ends the session. On the Mac, ⌘. also stops.
