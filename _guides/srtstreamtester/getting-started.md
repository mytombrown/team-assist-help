## What it is

- **SRT Tester** checks an SRT link and says what is in the stream.
- **Works on** iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- **Watch** a stream someone sends you, or **send** your camera and microphone.
- **Live numbers** show how healthy the link is.

## The guided setup

The app opens on a 4-step walk-through.

1. **Who connects to whom?** — tap **Caller**. You connect to an address someone gave you.
2. **Where** — pick what you want to do, then type the **Host** and **Port**.
3. **More options** — **Latency**, **Passphrase**, **Stream ID**. All optional.
4. **Ready** — check the summary, then press **Connect**.

- **Back** and **Next** move between steps.
- **Listener** is greyed out. It says **Coming in a future update**.

## Show all settings

- **Show all settings** (under the buttons) opens one flat form instead of the steps.
- **Use the guided setup** takes you back.

## The screen layout

- **iPhone:** one screen at a time — setup, then the session.
- **iPad and Mac:** setup stays in a sidebar. The session fills the rest.
- **While a session runs**, the setup is locked. The button becomes **Stop**.

## About and version

- **iPhone and iPad:** tap the ⓘ button on the setup screen.
- **Mac:** app menu › **About SRT Tester**.
- **Licenses** lists the open-source software the app uses.
