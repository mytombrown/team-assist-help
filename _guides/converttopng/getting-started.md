## What it does

- **Makes PNG copies** of your image files.
- **Keeps the size.** Images are not resized.
- **Keeps transparency.** See-through areas stay see-through.
- **Leaves the originals alone.** Your source files are not changed.
- **Runs on a Mac.**

## The main window

- **Top bar:** the title **Image to PNG Converter** and three buttons: **Start**, **Reset Failed** and **Clear**.
- **Left side:** counts for **Queued**, **Converted** and **Failed**.
- **Output:** the folder your PNGs go to, a **Choose Folder** button and a **Replace existing files** switch.
- **Accepted:** the file endings the app takes.
- **Drop area:** **Drop source images here**, with a **Browse Files** button.
- **Destination:** the full path of the output folder.
- **Source Formats:** the image types it reads.
- **Files:** every image you added, with its status.

## Your first conversion

1. **Drop images** on **Drop source images here**, or click **Browse Files**.
2. **Wait a moment.** Conversion starts on its own.
3. **Check the Files list.** Each row says **Completed** or **Failed**.
4. **Open the output folder** shown under **Destination** to find your PNGs.
