## Pick a folder

1. **Click Choose Folder** under **Output**. You can also click the **Destination** panel.
2. **Pick a folder.** You can make a new one in the same window.
3. **Click Choose.**

## Good to know

- **The full path** shows under **Destination**.
- **Remembered:** the app keeps your folder for next time.
- **Before you pick one,** PNGs go to a folder named **Converted PNGs**. Its path shows under **Destination**.

## Replace existing files

- **Off (default):** a new PNG never overwrites an old one. It gets a number, like `photo 2.png`.
- **On:** a new PNG with the same name overwrites the old one.
