## Build the list

- **Plan meals for the week first.**
- **Open the Groceries tab.**
- **Tap Build Grocery List.**
- **Ingredients come from the week's planned meals.**
- **The same ingredient in more than one meal** is combined into one line.

## Shop

- **Tap an item** to check it off. Tap again to uncheck.
- **The header** shows how many are left, or **All done!**

## Change the week or update the list

- **Arrows** at the top pick which week to shop for.
- **Changed the plan?** Tap **Refresh from plan**.
- **Items you already checked** stay checked after a refresh.

## Shared lists

- **If your family shares the plan,** everyone sees the same list. See [sharing with family]({{ '/familydinnerplans/share-with-family/' | relative_url }}).
