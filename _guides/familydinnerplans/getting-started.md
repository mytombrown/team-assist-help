Family Dinner Plans helps your family pick meals for the week, build a grocery list, and share it all.

## First launch

- **Welcome screen:** it asks which meals your family plans.
- **Turn on what you plan:** **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner**. Dinner is on to start.
- **Tap Get Started.** You need at least one meal turned on.
- **Change it later:** **Settings** > **Meal Slots**.

## The four tabs

- **Meals:** your family's meal list. Add, scan, search, edit.
- **Plan:** the week, one card per day. Tap a slot to pick a meal.
- **Groceries:** a shopping list built from the week's plan.
- **Settings:** meal slots, **Family Sharing**, **Calendar**, and the app **Version**.
