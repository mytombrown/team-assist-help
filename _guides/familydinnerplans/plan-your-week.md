## Pick a meal for a day

- **Open the Plan tab.** Each day has a card.
- **Tap a slot** (**Add a meal**, or **Add dinner** if you plan more than one meal).
- **Pick a meal** from the list. Use **Search meals** to find it.
- **Change it:** tap the slot again and pick another meal.
- **Remove it:** tap the **x** on the slot.

## Move between weeks

- **Arrows** at the top go to the previous or next week.
- **This week** shows under the dates when you're on the current week.

## Get meal ideas

- **Tap Suggest Meals** (the sparkles button).
- **It picks from your own meals** to fill the empty slots, with a reason for each.
- **Tap Add** on the ones you want. Tap **Done** when finished.
- **Only on devices with Apple Intelligence.** Otherwise the button does not show.

## Put the week on your calendar

- **First, pick a calendar:** **Settings** > **Calendar** > **Connect Calendar**, then choose one.
- **A shared family calendar** lets everyone see it.
- **Set times:** **Breakfast time**, **Lunch time**, **Dinner time**.
- **On the Plan tab, tap Add to Calendar.** It adds the week's meals.
- **After that,** new meals you plan go on the calendar right away.
- **The calendar and times are set on each phone,** not shared.
