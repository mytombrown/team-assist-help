## Invite your family

- **Open Settings.**
- **Under Family Sharing, tap Invite Family.**
- **Send the invite** with the share screen that opens.
- **They open the invite** on their iPhone to join.

## What they can do

- **See and edit** meals, the weekly plan, and the grocery list.
- **Meal Slots** are the same for everyone.
- **Calendar settings** are not shared. Each person picks their own calendar.

## See who has joined

- **Settings > Family Sharing** lists each person.
- **Owner:** the person who shared.
- **Joined:** accepted the invite.
- **Invited:** has not accepted yet.

## Change who is in

- **Tap Manage Sharing** to add or remove people, or stop sharing.
