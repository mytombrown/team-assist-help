## Add a meal by hand

- **Open the Meals tab.**
- **Tap Add Meal** (the **+** button).
- **Type the Meal name.** This is the only thing you must fill in.
- **Photo:** **Take Photo** or **Choose Photo**. Optional.
- **Time & Servings:** set the time in minutes and how many it serves.
- **Ingredients:** fill in **Qty**, **Unit**, **Ingredient**. Tap **Add Ingredient** for more rows.
- **Recipe & Cooking Instructions:** type the steps.
- **Tap Save.**

## Scan a recipe

- **In the Meals tab, tap Scan Recipe.**
- **Pick Take Photo or Choose Photo.**
- **Wait for "Reading recipe…".**
- **A New Meal form opens** with the name, ingredients and steps filled in.
- **Check it, fix anything wrong, tap Save.**

## Photo of a recipe page as a meal photo

- **In a meal's photo section,** a photo that looks like recipe text gets a question.
- **Extract Ingredients & Instructions:** fills the form from the photo.
- **Use as Meal Photo:** keeps it as the picture only.

## Edit or delete a meal

- **Edit:** tap the meal, then tap **Edit**.
- **Delete:** swipe left on the meal in the list.
- **Deleting a meal** also removes it from any planned days, and their calendar events.

## Find a meal

- **Use Search meals** at the top of the Meals list.
