## Calendar problems

- **"Calendar access is off":** open iPhone **Settings** > **Apps** > **Family Dinner Plans** and allow Calendars.
- **"Choose a calendar first":** in the app, go to **Settings** > **Calendar** and pick one.
- **"Couldn't write to the calendar":** pick a different calendar you can edit, then try **Add to Calendar** again.

## Scanning problems

- **"Couldn't read any text in that photo":** take a sharper photo, straight on, in good light.
- **No Take Photo option:** this device has no camera. Use **Choose Photo**.
- **Camera blocked:** iPhone **Settings** > **Apps** > **Family Dinner Plans**, allow Camera.
- **Scan got something wrong:** fix it in the form before tapping **Save**.

## Suggest Meals problems

- **No Suggest Meals button:** it needs a device with Apple Intelligence.
- **Button greyed out:** add meals first, and leave at least one empty slot this week.
- **"No Suggestions":** add more meals to your list.

## Sharing problems

- **"Sharing Problem" or "iCloud couldn't save the share":** make sure you're signed in to iCloud, then try **Invite Family** again.
- **Family can't see changes:** everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud.

## Grocery list is empty

- **Plan meals for that week first,** then tap **Build Grocery List**.
- **Check the week** with the arrows at the top.
