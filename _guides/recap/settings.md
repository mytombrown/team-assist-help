## What matters to me

- **Type what you care about** — for example "Decisions, owners, dates."
- **Every summary and newscast** puts this first.

## AI

- **Writes your notes** — **Apple Intelligence**, **Claude** or **ChatGPT**.
- **Apple Intelligence** runs on your device, free. Nothing leaves it.
- **Claude or ChatGPT** writes fuller notes. Paste your own API key, then **Save**.
- **With Claude or ChatGPT**, your transcript is sent to that company. They bill your account.
- **Your key** stays in this device's Keychain.

## Sync with iCloud

- **On:** recordings, notes, photos and podcasts appear on every device on your Apple Account.

## Voices

- **Natural voices** — tap **Download natural voices (327 MB)** once. Use Wi-Fi.
- **Use natural voices** — then pick the **Anchor**, **Host** and **Guest**.
- **Interview voices** — Apple voices for **Host** and **Guest**.
- **Get better voices** — steps to download Zoe and Evan, two free Apple voices.
- **Newscast voice** — the **Anchor** reads the newscast and Daily Recap.
- **New voice?** Tap **Again** on a recording to hear it.

## Storage

- **Delete recordings after 30 days** — off unless you turn it on.
- **Keep forever** recordings are never deleted.
- **Mac:** **Recordings folder** › **Choose…** to save in a folder other apps can read. **Use the default** to undo.
