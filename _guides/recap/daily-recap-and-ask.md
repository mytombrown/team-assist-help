## Daily Recap

1. **Tap Daily Recap** on the home screen.
2. **Pick the day.**
3. **Tap Create Daily Recap.**
4. **Tap play** to listen. Headlines first, then each meeting as its own story.

- **Again** — make it once more.
- **Done** — close it.

## Ask

1. **Tap Ask** on the home screen.
2. **Type a question** in **Ask a question**. For example: "What did I agree to this week?"
3. **Tap the arrow.**

- **The answer** lists the recordings it came from. Tap one to open it.
- **Done** — close it.
