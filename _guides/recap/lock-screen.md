## What you see

- **Start a recording, then lock the phone.**
- **The lock screen shows** the status, the timer, and your highlight and photo counts.
- **The Dynamic Island shows** the status and the timer. Press and hold it for the buttons.

## The buttons

- **Pause / Resume** — the timer stops while paused. Nothing is recorded.
- **Highlight** — marks this part of the conversation. It leads the notes.
- **Photo** — opens the camera over the lock screen. The photo goes into the recording.
- **Stop** — saves. The recording's page is there when you unlock.

## Photo from the lock screen

- **Allow the camera once** in Recap. Recap asks when you start a recording.
- **The phone has a passcode:** the camera opens over the lock screen. Lock again when done.
- **No passcode, or unlocked:** Recap opens with its camera.
- **Recap Photo control:** add it to the lock screen or Control Center for the same camera.

## Don't see it?

- **Settings › Recap › Live Activities** must be on.
- **First time:** tap **Allow** when iOS asks to allow Live Activities from Recap.
