## It won't record

- **"Recap needs the microphone."** Turn it on in Settings › Privacy › Microphone.
- **Mac, call audio:** allow Recap in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, then reopen Recap. Or turn off **Include call audio**.
- **Watch:** allow the microphone for Recap in Watch Settings.

## No transcript

- **"Recap needs Speech Recognition."** Turn it on in Settings › Privacy › Speech Recognition. Then tap Create again.
- **"No speech was heard in that recording."** Nothing to transcribe.
- **"This device can't transcribe on its own."** Recap never sends audio off the device.

## No podcast or notes

- **"This device doesn't support Apple Intelligence."** Recap can't make a podcast on it.
- **"Turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings."**
- **"Apple Intelligence is still downloading."** Try again in a little while.
- **"The API key was not accepted."** Check the key in Settings › AI.
- **"The AI service is busy or out of credit."** Try again shortly.

## The voices sound robotic

- **Tap Get voices** when asked, or Settings › **Get better voices**.
- **Or download natural voices** in Settings.
- **"The … voice isn't on this device."** Get voices in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.

## Recordings missing on another device

- **Check Settings › Sync with iCloud** is on.
- **Sign in to iCloud** on every device, with the same Apple Account.
- **Give it a moment.** New files download on their own.

## Natural voices won't download

- **"The download didn't finish."** Check the connection and try again. Use Wi-Fi.
