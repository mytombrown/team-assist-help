## Record

1. **Tap the round button** on the home screen.
2. **The recording screen opens.** The timer and voice wave show it is working.
3. **Tap Stop** when you are done.
4. **The recording's page opens.** Its title is the date and time — tap it to rename.

## While it records

- **Highlight** — marks this moment.
- **Photo** — snap a whiteboard or slide. Recap reads the text.
- **Your notes** — type names, decisions, to-dos. Your notes lead the summary.
- **Meeting type** (top right) — **General**, **1:1**, **Sales call**, **Stand-up**, **Lecture** or **Interview**. It shapes the notes.

## Record a call on the Mac

- **Settings › Include call audio** records your mic and the Mac's sound together.
- **The transcript** marks **You** and **Them**.
- **First time:** allow Recap in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.

## Record on Apple Watch

- **Open Recap on the Watch** and tap the button.
- **Tap again** to stop.
- **It sends** the recording to your iPhone. It shows **Sending to iPhone…** until done.

## Use Siri

- **"Start a Recap recording"** or **"Record with Recap"**.
