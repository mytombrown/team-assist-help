## Groups

- **Make a group:** tap the **folder pill** at the top of the list, then **Manage Groups… › New Group**.
- **Move a recording:** swipe it right, or press and hold it, then **Move to Group**.
- **See one group:** tap the folder pill and pick it. **All recordings** shows everything.
- **Rename or delete:** in **Manage Groups…**, press and hold a group.
- **Deleting a group keeps its recordings.** They go back to All recordings.

## Find a recording by what was said

- **Type words from the meeting** in the search box at the top of the list — like **unicorn pizza**.
- **Each result shows** the recording and the time it was said.
- **Tap a result:** the recording opens and plays from that moment.
- **No exact match?** Recordings with most of your words show as **Close match**.

## With Siri and Spotlight

- **Say "Find a Recap recording".** Siri asks what was said.
- **Spotlight** finds recordings by their transcript too.
