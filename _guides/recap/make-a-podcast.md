## Create it

1. **Open a recording** from the home screen.
2. **Pick a style:** **Newscast** (one voice, news stories) or **Interview** (Host and Guest).
3. **Tap Create newscast** or **Create podcast**.
4. **Watch the steps:** **Transcript**, **Summary and script**, **Voice**.

## Play it

- **Tap the play button** in the bar.
- **Again** — make it once more, for example after adding notes or new voices.
- **From** — tap **Summary** or **Transcript** to see where it got its info.

## Key points

- **Key points** appear at the top once it is made.
- **Tabs** switch between key points and each summary section.
- **Tap a point** to hear that moment in the recording.

## Add notes and photos after

- **Your notes and photos** — type notes or tap **Add photo**.
- **Then tap Again** to fold them into the summary.

## The files

- **Recording**, the podcast and **Transcript** are tiles. Tap one to open it.
- **Files** lists the rest, such as **Summary** and the script.
- **Mac:** **Show in Finder** opens the folder.

## Keep it

- **Keep forever** stops it being auto-deleted after 30 days.

## Use Siri

- **"Play the podcast of** *recording* **in Recap"**.
- **"Read the summary of** *recording* **in Recap"**.
- **"Read the transcript of** *recording* **in Recap"**.
