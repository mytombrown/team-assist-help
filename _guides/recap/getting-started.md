## What it is

- **Recap** records audio — a meeting, a call, a class.
- **It writes** a transcript and a summary.
- **It reads it back** as a **Newscast** or a two-voice **Interview**.
- **Works on** iPhone, iPad and Mac, with Apple Intelligence.
- **By default** it all runs on your device.

## First launch

- **Tap the round button** at the bottom to record.
- **Allow** the microphone when asked.
- **It keeps going** when the phone locks.

## The home screen

- **Your recordings** — every recording, newest first.
- **Grouped** by **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, then by month.
- **Each card** shows the title, length and a one-line gist.
- **Daily Recap** — one newscast of a whole day.
- **Ask** — ask a question across all your recordings.
- **Gear button** — **Settings**. On the Mac, use the app menu › Settings.

## Delete a recording

- **Swipe left** on it and tap **Delete**. On the Mac, right-click.
- **This removes** the audio, transcript, summary and podcast.
