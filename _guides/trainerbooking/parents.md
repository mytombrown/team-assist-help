## Add your trainer

- **Tap Add a trainer.**
- **Point your camera at the trainer's code,** or type it.
- **Tap See schedule.**

## Your info

- **My info and athletes:** your name, phone and email, and each athlete with their age.
- **Training for yourself?** Put your own name as the athlete too.

## Book

- **Book a Session:** pick a time, pick who's training, tap **Book it**.
- **Full** means every spot is taken.
- **Training Schedule** shows the next two weeks.

## Events and 1-on-1s

- **Special Events → Request a spot.** The trainer approves it.
- **Request a 1-on-1:** pick a time that suits you. The trainer approves it.

## Cancel

- **My bookings → Cancel booking.** The spot opens for someone else.
