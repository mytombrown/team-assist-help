## What it is

- **Trainers list sessions. Parents book them.**
- **Parents scan your code** to see your schedule.
- **No payments between you and parents.** Prices are shown only. Payment happens outside the app.

## Set up

- **Tap I'm a trainer.**
- **Fill in Set up your account.** Your name is what parents see.
- **Tap Create my account.**

## Add sessions

- **Schedule tab → Sessions → Add.**
- **Pick group or private,** ages, days, start times and length.
- **Group size** caps a group. A filled spot shows **Full**.
- **Location:** search Maps for the address. Venues you used show under **Recent venues**.
- **Price** is optional and shown to parents.

## Special events and 1-on-1s

- **Special events:** camps, clinics, tryouts. Parents send a request. You **Approve** or **Decline** on the **Requests** tab.
- **1-on-1s:** turn on **Parents can request a 1-on-1.** Parents pick a time. **Approve** puts it on your schedule.

## Your code

- **My Code** shows a code to scan, and the code as text.
- **Booking alerts on this phone** turns on a notice for each booking, cancel and request.

## Bookings and reports

- **Bookings** lists who is coming to each session.
- **Reports** shows day, week or month. **Export** makes a spreadsheet file.

## Your plan

- **The first 30 days are free.** Then $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year.
- **Parents never pay** for the app.
- **My Code → Your plan** shows your plan.
- **If your plan ends,** parents still see your page but cannot book. Bookings shows a red banner. Tap **Subscribe**.

## Updating from an earlier build

- **Your page moves to the new server** the first time you open this build.
- **Bookings made before stay** where they were. Ask parents to book again.
- **Android parents can now book** with you.

## Take an athlete off a session

- **Bookings → swipe left on the athlete → Deny.**
- **Type a reason.** Deny stays off until you do.
- **The spot opens** for someone else.
- **The parent gets a notice** with your reason.
