## What it is

- **A telling-time game** for kids 4 to 12.
- **iPhone and iPad.**
- **No ads. Nothing online. No accounts.**

## First launch

- **Pick a player** on the first screen, or make one.
- **The age sets where you start.** Older kids skip the easy days.

| Age | Shown under the age | Starts at |
|---|---|---|
| Under 6 | **O'clock and half past** | Level 1 |
| 6–7 | **Quarters and five minutes** | Level 3 |
| 8–9 | **To the minute, elapsed time** | Level 5 |
| 10+ | **Digital and rush days** | Level 7 |

## The game screen

- **Top left: the X.** Stops the game. Points so far are saved.
- **Level.** Your level now.
- **Score**, with **Stop 1 of 6** under it.
- **Flame.** How many right in a row.
- **Coins.** Your coins.
- **The town.** Six stops on a trail. The sun crosses the sky. The moon comes out by dinner.
- **The stop card.** What this stop asks.
- **The clock.** Read it, or drag its hands.

## The six stops

- **Bus stop**, **School**, **Lunch**, **Park**, **Dinner**, **Bed**.
- **Six stops make one day.** A full day moves you up one level.
