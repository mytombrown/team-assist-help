## The ladder

| Level | What it asks |
|---|---|
| 1 | **O'clock** |
| 2 | **Half past** |
| 3 | **Quarter past, quarter to** |
| 4 | **Five minutes at a time** |
| 5 | **To the minute** |
| 6 | **How long until — elapsed time** |
| 7 | **Digital to hands, hands to digital** |
| 8+ | **Rush day! Every kind of stop, on the clock** |

## How close the hands must be

- **Level 1:** within 7 minutes.
- **Levels 2–3:** within 5 minutes.
- **Level 4:** within 2 minutes.
- **Level 5 and up:** exact.

## Elapsed time (level 6)

- **The clock shows the time now.**
- **The card says how long until.** Example: "The bus comes in 20 minutes."
- **Tap the time it will be.**

## Digital (level 7)

- **A digital time shows.** Set the hands to match.
- **Or read the hands** and pick the digital time.

## Rush days (level 8 and up)

- **A timer bar shows on every stop.** It turns red at 5 seconds.
- **24 seconds a stop on level 8.** 2 fewer each level after. Never under 10.
- **Time runs out = missed.** No points.

## Badges

- **Seven ladders:** **Stops reached**, **Perfect days**, **In a row**, **Hardest level**, **All-time points**, **Rush days**, **Hands set exactly**.
- **New badges show** at the end of the day.
