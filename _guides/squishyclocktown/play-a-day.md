## Pick the time

- **Read the clock.** The card asks what time it says.
- **Tap one of the four times** under the clock.

## Set the hands

- **The card names a time.** Example: "The bus comes at half past 8."
- **Drag a hand.** Grab the one nearest your finger.
- **Short hand = hour.** Long hand = minutes.
- **Or use the arrows.** **Hour** and **Minute** each have − and + buttons.
- **The time you set shows** between the arrows.
- **Tap Set!** when it is right.

## Right, wrong, missed

- **Right first try:** **Right on time!** You get 5 × your level in points.
- **Right on the second try:** **Made it!** Same points.
- **First miss:** **Hmm, not quite.** You see a hint. Tap **Try again**.
- **Second miss:** **Oh no — missed it!** The squishy flops. You see the answer. No points.
- **Tap Next stop** to go on.

## End of the day

- **After Bed, the day is done.** You see **Day … done!**
- **Bonus:** 25 × your level.
- **A star** if every stop was right on the first try.
- **Coins and new badges** show here as **Achievement: …**.
- **Tomorrow:** what the next level is about.
- **Next day** goes up one level.
- **Stop for now** ends the game.
