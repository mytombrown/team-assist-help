## My answer was close but marked wrong

- **From level 5 the hands must be exact.**
- **Use the − and + arrows** to set the last minute.
- **Check the short hand too.** It must point at the right hour.

## The hands won't drag

- **They lock while a message is up.** Tap the button on the message first.
- **Use the arrows instead.** **Hour** and **Minute** each have − and +.

## The timer is too fast

- **The timer starts at level 8.** It gets shorter each level.
- **It never goes under 10 seconds.**

## It's too hard or too easy

- **The start level comes from the player's age.**
- **Each finished day moves up one level.**

## Did I lose my points?

- **Points are saved** when a day ends, when you tap **Stop for now**, or when you tap the X.
