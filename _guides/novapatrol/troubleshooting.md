## No sound

- **Check the silent switch** and the volume.

## A mission is too hard

- **Shoot the gold Carrier.** It drops a Shield.
- **Save the Nova Bomb** for a crowded screen or a boss attack.
- **Keep your power-ups.** A hit takes them; dodge first, shoot second.

## A galaxy is locked

- **Finish the galaxy before it.** Then the next one opens.

## My score is not on Game Center

- **Sign in:** Settings › Game Center. Then open the game again.
- **Finish a mission.** Your total is sent after each one.
- **No High Scores button?** You are not signed in to Game Center.

## My progress is not on my new phone

- **Same Apple ID?** Both phones must use it.
- **iCloud on?** Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Nova Patrol must be allowed.
- **Open the game on both phones.**
- **Deleting the app** deletes this phone's copy. Your iCloud copy stays until you remove it.

## Contact support

- **We collect nothing.** Your total can go to Apple's Game Center if you sign in. Progress goes to your own iCloud.
