## What it is

- **A 16-bit style space shooter.** On iPhone.
- **Free. No ads. No purchases.**
- **50 missions in 5 galaxies.** 10 bosses.
- **Progress follows you.** Scores, stars and credits save to your own iCloud. Another iPhone on the same Apple ID picks up where you left off.
  - **iCloud off** for the app: progress stays on this phone only.
- **Game Center:** signed in? Your total goes on the High Scores board. Not signed in: the game plays the same.

## Start playing

- **Tap Play.** Pick a galaxy, then a planet.
- **Drag with one thumb.** The ship fires by itself.
- **Your hull takes 3 hits.** A hit also takes your power-ups.

## Power-ups

- **Each has 3 levels.** Catch it again to level up.
- **> Speed Up (from mission 1):** the ship moves further than your thumb.
- **R Rapid Fire (from mission 2):** faster, then twin bolts, then three.
- **W Wing Drones (from mission 4):** one drone, then two, then they fire faster.
- **S Shield:** shot down the gold Carrier? Catch it. 9 seconds of cover.
- **Nova Bomb (from mission 3):** the button bottom-left. Clears the screen, once a mission.
- **Lost one to a hit?** It drops again — unless the mission is almost done.

## The galaxies

- **Helios Run:** the basics. Drones, darts, fighters, gunships, asteroids, gold carriers.
- **Ember Nebula:** mines. They burst into shots if you let them get close.
- **Frost Veil:** seekers. They steer at you.
- **Hive Cluster:** lancers. They park up top, blink, then fire one fast shot.
- **Void Core:** warpers. They jump from place to place.
- **Finish a galaxy** to open the next one.
- **Later galaxies are harder**, not longer.

## Bosses

- **Two in every galaxy:** at missions 5 and 10.
- **Helios Run:** The Warden, Void Marshal.
- **Ember Nebula:** Cinder, Forge Titan.
- **Frost Veil:** Icebreaker, Glacier Queen.
- **Hive Cluster:** Hive Guard, Hive Mother.
- **Void Core:** Eclipse, Singularity.
- **At half health** a boss gets angry.

## Stars and score

- **Up to 3 stars** a mission.
- **Score = ships + time + credits + hull.**
  - **Ships:** every ship you shoot down.
  - **Time:** under par scores more.
  - **Credits:** the gold you collect.
  - **Hull:** hits you have left.
- **Total score** = each mission's best, added up.
- **High Scores** on the title screen opens the Game Center board. It only shows when you are signed in.
