## What it is

- **A shadow puzzle.** On iPhone.
- **Two lamps, two walls, two pictures.** Build so both shadows match.
- **Five worlds, 20 rooms each.** Each world adds one new rule.
- **Tonight's room:** one new room every day.

## Start playing

- **Rooms 1 and 2** walk you through it. From room 3 you're on your own.
- **Each lamp shines** at the wall across from it.
- **Tap a floor square** to add a block. Blocks cast shadows on both walls.
- **Few blocks.** One tall stack can fill both walls at once.
- **Tap Lights on** to check.

## Hearts and coins

- **A wrong Lights on** costs a heart. Your build stays.
- **Up to 5 hearts.** One comes back every 30 minutes.
- **Coins come from playing.** Hint: 30 coins. Extra block: 50 coins.

## The worlds

- **Classic** — the basic rules.
- **Stuck blocks** — grey blocks can't move.
- **Holes** — nothing stands on a dark square.
- **Glass** — glass casts no shadow.
- **Third light** — a lamp overhead. Match the floor too.

## The shop

- **Coin packs:** Candle, Lantern, Street Lamp, Lighthouse.
- **Night Owl Kit:** coins, 4 hints, 4 extra blocks. One time only.
- **1 Hour Endless Hearts.** Buy again to add an hour.
- **New phone?** Tap **Restore purchases** in the shop.

## Scores

- **Game Center is optional.** Signed in, your scores go on the boards and trophies show as achievements.
