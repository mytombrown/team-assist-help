## Find it on the network

- **Open Connect.**
- **Look under Found on network.** It says **Looking for Companion…** while it searches.
- **Tap a name** to connect.
- **Companion 5** announces itself by name.
- **Companion 4** shows as **Companion at** plus its IP address.
- **Keeps looking.** The list refreshes every 20 seconds while Connect is open.

## Type the address

- **Host** — Companion's IP address.
- **Port** — Companion's Satellite WebSocket port. The app starts with **16623**.
- **Surface Name** — how this phone shows up in Companion.
- **Tap Connect.** Tap **Disconnect** to stop.

## It remembers you

- **Free.** No purchase needed.
- **Last Connection** — the card on Connect shows the Companion you used last.
- **Connects on launch.** Turn this off in **Settings** → **Connection** → **Auto-connect last**.
- **Auto reconnect** — tries again if the link drops. On by default.

## More than one Companion

- **Save Server** keeps more than one. That is **Multi-Instance**.
