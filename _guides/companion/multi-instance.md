## What you get

- **Saved servers** — keep every Companion you use.
- **Switch on the Surface page** — change Companion without leaving the buttons.
- **iPad: two surfaces at once** — two Companions side by side.
- **One-time purchase.** Not a subscription.

## What stays free

- **Connecting.**
- **Your last connection,** remembered and used when the app opens.
- **Dashboard and Operator.**

## Buy it

- **Settings** → **Purchases** → tap **Multi-Instance**.
- **Or tap any lock icon** — **Save Server**, the lock by the Companion's name, or **Second surface**.
- **The price** shows on the button.

## Saved servers

- **Tap Save Server** on Connect.
- **Servers** card lists them.
- **Bolt** — connect. **Open** — load its settings. **Star** — favorite.
- **Copy icon** — duplicate. **Trash** — delete.
- **Rename** — type in the name box.
- **Favorite connects on launch** when **Auto-connect favorite/last** is on.

## Switch Companion

- **On the Surface page,** tap the Companion's name.
- **Pick one** under **Saved** or **On this network**.

## Two surfaces on iPad

- **Tap Second surface** on the Surface page.
- **Tap the name** on the new side to pick its Companion.
- **Tap the ✕** on the second side to go back to one.

## Bought it before?

- **Tap Restore Purchases** in **Settings** → **Purchases**.
- **Old saved servers** come back once Multi-Instance is on.
