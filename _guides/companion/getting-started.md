## What it is

- **A remote surface.** Your phone or iPad shows Bitfocus Companion's buttons.
- **Same network.** The phone and Companion must be on the same local network.
- **Free to use.** Connecting and remembering your connection cost nothing.

## First launch

- **Allow Local Network.** iOS asks once. Say yes, or the app can't find Companion.
- **Open Connect.** It is the first tab.
- **Tap your Companion** under **Found on network**.
- **Or type it in.** Enter **Host** and **Port**, then tap **Connect**.
- **Keys appear** on the **Surface** tab once you are **Online**.

## The tabs

- **Connect** — find a Companion and connect.
- **Surface** — Companion's buttons.
- **Dashboard** — your own picks of keys, from any page.
- **Settings** (gear icon) — app, connection, surface and diagnostics settings.
- **Status pill** — next to the tabs: **Online**, **Connecting** or **Offline**.

## The Surface page

- **Arrows** — previous or next Companion page.
- **Page line** — shows the page number under the Companion's name.
- **Rotate button** — turns the buttons upright (vertical layout) or back.
- **Rotate to Landscape** — shown on a phone held upright. Turn it, or tap **Vertical Layout**.

## Change the buttons

All in **Settings** → **Surface**:

- **Rows** and **Columns** — the size of the grid.
- **Artwork** — **Off**, **72** or **96**. Off is fastest.
- **Text Size** — make key text bigger or smaller.
- **Advanced layout** — turns on **Encoders** (dials).
- **Variables** — sends Companion this phone's connection status and battery level.
- **Press feedback** — show a press on the key.
- **Haptic on press** — a tap you can feel (iPhone).

## App settings

In **Settings** → **App**:

- **Theme** — **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Keep screen awake while connected** — on by default.
