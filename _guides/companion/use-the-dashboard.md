## What it is

- **Your own page.** Keys from any Companion page, on one screen.
- **Live.** The keys show what Companion draws and press like normal keys.

## Add keys

- **Connect first.** Offline, it says **Connect to Companion first.**
- **Open Dashboard.**
- **Tap Add keys.**
- **Use the arrows** to move between pages.
- **Tap a key** to add it. Tap again to take it off.
- **Tap Done.**

## Remove keys

- **Tap Edit.**
- **Tap the minus** on a key.
- **Tap Done.**

## Needs Companion 5

- **Satellite subscriptions** must be on in Companion's settings.
- **Without it** the app says **This Companion can't share keys with a Dashboard.**

## Share it

- **Tap Share** to give some keys to another phone.
- See [Operator mode]({{ '/companion/share-keys-operator/' | relative_url }}).
