This is the Home tab when you use Team Assist as a parent or an adult athlete
(the **Athlete** profile). The bar at the bottom has three tabs: **Home**,
**Teams**, and **Settings**.

## The top of Home

- **Your name**, with a line under it naming the athlete and how many teams they
  are on — for example "Bench · 2 teams".
- **Today** — a card with how many sessions there are today and their times. The
  capsule on the right says **All clear** in green, or **N need attention** in
  orange when something is waiting on you.
- **Team chips** — **All teams**, then one chip per coach or trainer. Tap one to
  show only that coach's items.
- The **profile button** is top-left. The **chat bubble** and the **bell** are
  top-right; the bell shows a count of unread notifications.

## Needs Attention

Anything that needs an answer lands here: payment requests, package warnings,
assignments, and availability. Each row shows what it is, who sent it, the amount
and due date if it is money, and a chip naming the athlete.

- **Show all** opens the full list when only the first few are showing.
- The **×** on a row dismisses it. Dismissed rows are counted underneath —
  "1 dismissed · Restore" — and **Restore** brings them back.
- When there is nothing to do, the section reads **Nothing needs action**.

## Upcoming

The next sessions, one row each: the day and time, the session name, which
coach or trainer runs it, and a chip naming the athlete. **View all** opens the
full list. Tap a row to open the session.

## The four tiles

Under Upcoming are four tiles. Each carries a count and a one-line summary:

- **Assignments** — work a coach has sent, with how many need attention.
- **Announcements** — team messages, with how many are unread.
- **Packages** — the athlete's session packages, with credits left.
- **Finance** — what is owed, shown as an outstanding total, and your payment
  history. See [packages, credits and what you owe]({{ '/family-packages/' | relative_url }}).
