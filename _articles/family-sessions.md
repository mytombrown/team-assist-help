## Where sessions are

- **Home → Upcoming** lists the next sessions. **View all** opens the full list.
- **Teams → Sessions** lists them under the athlete, with its own **View all**.
- **View all** opens the **Future Sessions** sheet, split into **Current & Future**
  and **Past Sessions**. Past sessions show the last year only.

## Reading a session card

Every session is one card:

- the **day** on the left (for example "THU 3"),
- the **session name**, then the time range,
- which hat runs it and the coach's name — for example "Coach · Bench B Coach",
- the **location**, with a pin. Tap it for directions.

## Answering

- A session waiting on you shows a blue **New** capsule and two buttons:
  green **Accept**, and **Decline**.
- Tap one. The buttons disappear and the capsule shows your answer — for example
  a green **✓ Accepted**.
- The coach sees your answer on their phone. If you decline, they see the reason
  you give.

## Changing your answer

Tap the card to open the session. Your answer can be changed there until the
session starts. **Add to Calendar** lives on the same screen.
