Your coach connects one phone — yours — with an invite code. After that, you can
add the other parent's or guardian's phone to the same connection. It sees the same
coach, the same sessions and the same chat. Nobody needs a new code from the coach.

## Who is who

Each phone in a family has a role:

- **Owner** — the phone that used the coach's invite code. It can add phones and
  remove any other phone.
- **Parent** — a phone added from the owner's QR. It can add phones too, and remove
  any phone except the owner's.
- **Athlete** — the athlete's own phone, for athletes 15 or older. It gets the
  schedule and chat. It does not see packages or balances, and cannot add or
  remove phones.

The other phone can be an iPhone or an Android phone.

## Adding a parent's phone (on your phone)

1. Open the **Teams** tab.
2. Under **Coaches**, find your coach's row and tap the **Family** button (the
   two-people icon). The **Family** screen opens and lists the phones already
   on it.
3. Under **Add a phone**, tap **Add parent**.
4. A QR code appears, with a code under it that looks like **ABCD-EFGH-JKMN**,
   and a timer: **Expires in 14:59**.
5. Keep this screen open while the other phone scans it.

To add the athlete's own phone instead, turn on **(athlete's name) is 15 or older**
and tap **Add athlete**. The QR works the same way.

The code works once, for 15 minutes. If the timer runs out it reads
**Expired — make a new one**: tap **Done** and tap **Add parent** again.

## Joining (on the other parent's phone)

Install Team Assist on that phone first and set it up as a parent.

1. Open the **Teams** tab.
2. If this phone has no coach yet, the **Connect a coach** card is already open.
   If it has one, tap the **+** next to **Coaches** (the row reads **Manage** and
   **+**).
3. At the bottom of the card, tap **Join a family with a QR**.
4. Tap **Scan QR** and point the camera at the QR on the other phone.
   Or type the code under **Or type the code** and tap **Join**.
5. It joins in a few seconds. The coach's sessions and chat start to appear.

On the first phone, the QR closes on its own once the new phone shows up. The
**Family** screen now lists it as **Parent** (or **Athlete**), with **Joined** and
the date.

## On the athlete's own phone (15 to 17)

When Team Assist first opens it asks your age. Choose **I am an athlete, 15 to 17**.
The phone opens as an athlete only, and the only way in is the athlete QR from
your parent's phone: open **Teams**, tap **Join a family with a QR**, and scan it.

A parent QR does not work on an athlete's phone. It reads **"This code is for a
parent. Ask your parent for an athlete code."** and the phone is not added to the
family. Under 15, the app does not open.

## Removing a phone

1. On the **Teams** tab, tap the **Family** button on your coach's row.
2. Tap **Remove** on the phone's row.
3. Tap **Remove** again to confirm.

That phone stops getting updates for your athlete, and the coach drops off it the
next time it opens. You will not see **Remove**
on your own phone's row (it is marked **This phone**). A parent phone cannot
remove the owner.

## If it does not work

- **"A family code is 12 letters and numbers."** — check the code and type it again.
- **"That QR is not a Team Assist family code."** — scan the QR on the **Family**
  screen, not another code.
- **"Camera is off for Team Assist. Type the code instead."** — type the code, or
  turn the camera on for Team Assist in the iPhone's Settings.
- **The code expired, or was already used** — tap **Add parent** for a new one.
