Open **Run Playbook** on a session and the recording starts on its own — a red
**Recording** state sits in the header, and there is no record button to find. The
first time, your phone asks for the microphone and for speech recognition; say yes to
both. (Decline the microphone and nothing is recorded; decline speech recognition and
the audio is kept but not transcribed.)

## When you end the run

Tap **End Run**. The screen closes and the phone gets to work:

1. **Transcribing.** The audio is turned into text on the phone. On phones that
   support on-device speech recognition the audio never leaves the phone; on older
   phones Apple's speech service does the transcription.
2. **Summarizing.** When the transcript is ready, **Apple Intelligence** writes a
   short session summary — no tap needed. On a phone without Apple Intelligence,
   Team Assist writes a plain summary itself and says so.
3. **Pulling each athlete's notes.** Apple Intelligence reads the transcript once more
   and picks out what you said about each athlete on the session — the athletes on the
   session plus the athletes on its teams. Names are matched to your roster: full
   names, first names, and common short forms ("Mike" for Michael). If two athletes
   share a first name, Team Assist does not guess — the name goes on a
   **Names to check** list, along with anyone you mentioned who is not on the roster.

This takes a minute or two. If you open the run again in the meantime, the Session
Summary line tells you where it is ("Summarizing with Apple Intelligence…", then
"Summary ready… Notes for 2 athletes.").

## Where it lands

Open the session. A **Recording** section shows:

- the status — **Draft — trainer only, not shared** until you act on it;
- the summary;
- a block for each athlete with what was said about them;
- **Names to check**, if any;
- when it was generated, and whether Apple Intelligence or the phone wrote it.

**Edit** opens the summary and every athlete's notes so you can fix anything before
anyone else sees it. Edits stay trainer-only.

## Approving

Tap **Approve and share with families**. Two things happen:

- the summary is shared with the connected families of the athletes on the session,
  through the same sharing Team Assist already uses for session summaries;
- each athlete's notes are written onto that athlete's record as a note dated to the
  session — "Recording note — Tuesday Skills, Sep 18, 2026" — so the athlete's page
  carries what you said, once.

Until you approve, nothing from the recording reaches a family. Approving is per
recording; a second tap never writes the athlete notes twice.

## Privacy

- **The audio never leaves your phone.** It is not uploaded, synced or shared.
- **The transcript, summary and notes are your Team Assist records.** They belong to
  you like the rest of your data, and only the approved summary is ever shared — and
  only with families connected to the athletes on that session.
- **Apple Intelligence runs on the phone.** The transcript is not sent to a server to be
  summarized.
