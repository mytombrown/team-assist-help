Team Assist is organised into tabs along the bottom of the screen: **Home**,
**Schedule**, **Athletes** (called **Teams** in the Coach profile), **Tools**, and
**Search**. The Home tab is the dashboard.

## What's on Home

- **Today** — today's sessions. Tap a session to open it, or **View all** for the full
  schedule. Before anything is scheduled it shows "All clear — no sessions scheduled
  today", which is the normal day-one state, not an error.
- **Needs Attention** — a follow-up queue with a count for each category, such as unpaid
  sessions, session confirmations, assignment completions, and announcement reads. Tap a
  category to work through it. The section only appears when something actually needs
  attention.
- **Workspace** — shortcuts into Schedule, Athletes, Teams, Assignments, Announcements,
  and Payments.

At the top there are buttons for **Messages** and alerts, and a profile pill for
switching between the Trainer, Coach, and Athlete profiles.

## Can it be customised?

The layout itself is fixed, but the Needs Attention queue is configurable: in **Tools →
Settings → Needs Attention** you can choose which follow-up categories feed the
dashboard queue, and individual items can be dismissed.

## The first time you open the app

Before you ever see the dashboard, Team Assist asks you to confirm you're 18 or over,
lets you pick a profile (Trainer, Coach, or Athlete), shows a short tour of how the app
works for that profile, and walks you through a guided setup. The dashboard appears once
setup is complete — initially with empty sections and the Workspace shortcuts, which is
where adding your first athletes and sessions starts.
