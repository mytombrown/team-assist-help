**Team Assist has three plans.** Every feature is in Starter. Pro is the same, with more room.

- **Free:** up to 3 active athletes.
- **Starter:** up to 25 active athletes.
- **Pro:** up to 250 active athletes.

Prices come from the App Store and show on the Subscription screen.

## See or change your plan

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Subscription**.
3. Your **Current plan** is marked. Tap **Upgrade** on the plan you want.

## Already paid on another phone?

On the Subscription screen, tap **Restore Purchases**.

## Cancel or change billing

On the Subscription screen, tap **Manage Subscription**. It opens your App Store subscriptions.

## Good to know

- **The athlete and parent app is always free.**
- **Moving to a smaller plan never deletes or hides a record.**
- **Early Access:** if you used Team Assist before plans arrived, you keep everything you had.
- **At your limit,** adding an athlete or switching an archived one back on asks you to upgrade.
