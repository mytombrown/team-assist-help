The first thing to know: **Team Assist records payments; it does not process them.** No
money moves through the app. You collect payment however you already do — cash, bank
transfer, a payment link from your own provider — and Team Assist keeps track of who
owes what and what's been paid.

The finance center (in the Tools tab) has six sections:

- **Balances** — athletes with open balances, overdue requests, and remaining package
  credits.
- **Payments** — recorded payments that count as paid history, with receipt records.
- **Packages** — session packages: their payment status, active credits, and any
  package-linked requests.
- **Requests** — requests you've sent for packages, sessions, or custom
  balances, with statuses like Draft, Sent, Waiting for Payment, and Paid.
- **Reports** — exports for payment totals, balances, package credits, and receipts.
- **Settings** — where you choose how you collect money.

## Packages and templates

A **package** is a block of sessions someone buys up front. You create a reusable
package, assign it to an athlete when they buy, and Team Assist tracks credits used and
credits remaining. **Templates** are reusable package definitions so you're not
rebuilding the same package each time.

**Credits come off when a session is settled.** Marking a session as settled against a
package takes one credit from that package, and the remaining credits on **Balances**
and **Packages** change to match. If you settle a session and the credit count does not
move, the two are out of step — that is worth reporting rather than adjusting by hand.

**Credits belong to the coach who sold the package.** A package bought from one coach or
trainer is spent on that person's sessions. If an athlete works with more than one
person, each one's packages are counted separately, and a credit from one does not pay
for the other's session.

## How payment collection works

In Settings you pick a payment mode:

- **Track Only** (the default) — Team Assist creates requests, balances, payment
  history, and package status. The connected parent or adult athlete is told to contact
  you for payment; you record what you receive and mark it paid.
- **Payment Links** — adds a Pay Now button that opens your own provider (a Venmo
  handle, PayPal, Square, Stripe, or a custom link you supply). The app itself puts it
  this way: "Payment links open your provider. You still confirm the money arrived, then
  mark the request paid in Team Assist."
- **Built-In Payments** — shown in Settings but not enabled: the app describes it as a
  future automated version, and the message shown for it today is "In-app payments are
  not enabled yet."

Whichever mode you use, marking something paid is your action, based on money you
actually received — the app never assumes.
