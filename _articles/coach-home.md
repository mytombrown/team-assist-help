As a coach, Team Assist has tabs along the bottom: **Home**, **Schedule**, **Teams**,
**Tools**, and **Search**. Home is the dashboard.

## What's on Home

- **Today** — today's practices and games. Tap one to open it, or **View all** for the
  full schedule. Before anything is scheduled it shows "All clear — no sessions scheduled
  today", which is the normal day-one state.

  Home shows a summary. On a busy day the list stops after the first few and tells you
  **how many more there are** — tap it for the full list on Schedule.
- **Needs Attention** — follow-ups with a count for each, such as session declines,
  availability requests, assignment completions, and announcement reads. It only appears
  when something actually needs attention.

  **Session Confirmations lists declines only.** A family that hasn't answered yet is
  not a follow-up; a decline shows up with their reason.
- **Workspace** — shortcuts into Schedule, Teams, and Assignments.

At the top are **Messages** and alerts, and a profile pill for switching profiles.

## Notifications

The bell opens the Notification Center. Each notification has **Open**, **Mark as
Read**, and **Delete**; swiping a row deletes it too. **Mark All as Read** clears the
count; **Delete All Notifications** empties the list after asking. Deleted stays deleted.

## Search

The **Search** tab searches your players, practices, games, teams and the rest of your
data as you type.

## Can it be customised?

In **Tools → Settings → Needs Attention** you choose which follow-ups feed the queue,
and single items can be dismissed.
