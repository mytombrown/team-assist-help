Everyone with an account is an adult, so every conversation in Team Assist is between
adults. For an athlete under 18 that means you're messaging their parent or guardian.

**Nobody under 18 appears in chat.** They have no account, so there's nothing to message.

## Conversations

Messages live behind the Messages button at the top of the screen. There are three kinds
of conversation:

- **Athlete conversations** — one-to-one with the connected parent or adult athlete for
  a particular athlete. These are marked "Athlete thread".
- **Team conversations** — a group conversation around a team.
- **Staff conversations** — with staff members you've added.

To start one, tap **New Message** and pick from *Teams and Groups* or *Athletes &
Parents*. Only people actually connected to you appear — chat follows connections, so
there's no directory to browse and nobody can message you out of the blue. Connected
parents and adult athletes can message you the same way from their side.

Some team conversations can be view-only for certain participants; if that's you, the
composer says "You can view this thread, but you do not have posting access." Each
conversation has an info button showing who's in it and who can post.

## What can be sent

Text and emoji only — the conversation says exactly that at the top. No photos, files,
or voice messages.

## Sending, and what the labels under a message mean

While a message is on its way it says **sending…** underneath. If it genuinely cannot
be delivered — for example the connection it belongs to needs a new invite — the label
changes to **Not delivered** with the reason, and the message stops pretending. Your
text stays visible either way, so nothing you wrote is lost.

When someone wears more than one hat — the same person is both a coach and a trainer,
say — each message shows which role it was sent as, so a conversation never leaves you
guessing who you're talking to.

## Editing, deleting, and history

Messages can't be edited or deleted after sending — what was said is what was said.

Your conversation history lives with your connection, not with any one phone. Sign in
on a new device and the conversation is there; delete and reinstall the app and it
comes back. That also means clearing a conversation from one device doesn't remove it
— the history returns the next time the conversation syncs.
