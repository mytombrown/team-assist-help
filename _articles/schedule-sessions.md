The Schedule tab is where sessions live, alongside the session builder, playbooks, and
locations.

## Creating a session

You build a session by choosing the session type, picking the date, time, and location,
and selecting the team or athletes taking part. You can attach a playbook if you want a
plan to run from, and reusable session templates fill in the repeated setup — you still
choose the new date and time before saving.

**Repeating sessions.** A session can repeat — the options are Does Not Repeat, Daily,
Weekdays (Mon–Fri), Weekly, Monthly, or Custom. Team Assist generates the series up
front (up to 30 sessions in a series).

## Editing and rescheduling

Rescheduling is editing: open the session and change its date or time. Deeper changes go
through Advanced Edit on the session.

## Cancelling and deleting

Deleting a session always asks first. For a repeating session you choose between
**Cancel This Session** and **Cancel All in Series**. The confirmation warns that
related payment records are removed along with the session.

There's also **Cancel and Message Group**, which removes the session and pre-fills a
text message to the people involved ("… has been canceled") so you're not typing the
same thing twice.

Team Assist takes a backup snapshot before deleting, and snapshots can be restored from
**Tools → Settings → Backup** — so an accidental deletion is recoverable.

## Who sees your sessions

Sessions appear automatically for the connected parent or adult athlete of each athlete
involved, and removing a session removes it for them too. If nobody is connected for an
athlete, nothing is shared — see
[who can see what]({{ '/athlete-visibility/' | relative_url }}).

## Your iPhone's Calendar

If you allow calendar access when asked, Team Assist adds sessions to your iPhone's
Calendar — with the time, location, and athlete names — updates the event when the
session changes, and removes it when the session is deleted. Declining access changes
nothing else; sessions just stay inside Team Assist.
