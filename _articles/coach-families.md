Everything goes to the adults connected to each player: a parent or guardian, or a
player 18 or over. A player 15 to 17 can also see it on their own phone once a parent
adds that phone to the family. Nothing goes to anyone under 15.

## What they get

- **Practices and games** that include their player. They appear when you save them and
  the family can **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **Assignments** — follow-up work they can mark complete.
- **Announcements** — team messages they can mark read. They last 10 days, then delete
  themselves on both phones.
- **Updates** you choose to share for a player.

If nobody is connected for a player, nothing is shared. That's normal.

## On their phone

They install Team Assist, choose the **Athlete** profile, and connect with your invite
code. Their Home opens on **Today**, then what needs an answer, then **Upcoming**. Each
practice or game is one card with **Accept** and **Decline**; tapping the place opens
directions. They can message you through
[team chat]({{ '/teamassist/chat-basics/' | relative_url }}).

## Taking something back

Delete a practice, assignment or announcement and it disappears from their phone too.
Disconnecting a family ends what they can see — see
[who can see what]({{ '/teamassist/athlete-visibility/' | relative_url }}).
