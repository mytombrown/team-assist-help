Staff — an assistant coach, a trainer who helps with a team, a team manager — join
Team Assist the same way a family does: with an invite code from you. There is no
staff account and nothing to sign up for. They install the app, enter the code and
their phone number, and they're connected.

## Inviting a staff member

1. On the **Teams** tab, switch the list to **Staff** and add the person (or open
   them if they're already there). Enter their **phone number** — the invite is tied
   to it.
2. Assign them to **at least one team**. A staff invite is always for a team; without
   one there is nothing to share.
3. Under **Team Assist Access**, tap **Invite to Team Assist**. Send them the
   10-character code — **Text Invite Code** does it in one tap.

Only a coach profile can invite staff. The invite can be deactivated later from the
same section; a deactivated invite stops sharing immediately.

## What they get

Once connected, a staff member sees, for the team they were invited to:

- **Sessions** — every practice, game, or session on that team's schedule, plus any
  session you put them on directly.
- **Announcements** — team announcements, as you send them.
- **Assignments** — assignments addressed to the team.
- **Chat** — a direct thread with you, and the team thread.

They see athlete **names**, so the roster reads properly. They do not see anything
that belongs to a family: guardian names and phone numbers, packages, balances,
payments, availability requests, or parent updates. That boundary is enforced on the
server, not just hidden on the screen.

## What they can't do

A staff member is a participant, like an athlete. They cannot edit the schedule,
change the roster, see finance, or invite anyone. Anything they need changed, they
ask you.

## Removing a staff member

Deactivate their invite from the **Team Assist Access** section, or mark them inactive.
Either stops the sharing; deleting the staff record removes them from the team lists
too.
