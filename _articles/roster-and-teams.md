There's one kind of record for a person you coach: the **athlete record**. Whether you
think of someone as a client, a roster entry, or a team member, it's the same record —
teams are just groupings of athlete records.

## The athlete record

An athlete record holds the athlete's name, the parent or guardian's name, phone number
and email (with room for a second guardian), notes, strengths and weaknesses, and an
optional internal skill level. Sessions, attendance, packages, payments, availability,
and progress history all hang off this record.

It stands on its own. It doesn't need a parent or adult athlete connected to be
complete — scheduling, teams, billing, and notes all work regardless. See
[inviting a parent or an adult athlete]({{ '/invitations/' | relative_url }}) for what
connecting someone adds.

## Teams

Teams group athletes for practices, games, rosters, and group updates. An athlete has a
primary team and can also be associated with additional teams, so being an individual
client and a team member at the same time is normal, as is being on several teams.
Trainer profiles can skip teams entirely and work one athlete at a time.

## Removing someone

There are two different actions, and the difference matters:

- **Archive** sets the athlete to Inactive. Nothing is deleted — the record and its
  whole history stay, marked Inactive, and **Restore Athlete** brings it back.
- **Delete Athlete** asks for confirmation and then removes the record along with its
  availability, packages, package usage, attendance, and requests. The athlete is also
  taken off their sessions; a session left with no athletes at all is removed too.

Team Assist takes a backup snapshot before a deletion, restorable from **Tools →
Settings → Backup**. If you only want someone off your active list, archive — it's
reversible; deletion is for when you genuinely want the record gone.
