## On Home

- **Needs Attention** carries each **payment request**: what it is for, who
  sent it, the amount, and the due date — for example "$250.00 · Due Sep 2".
- The **Packages** tile shows how many credits are left across the athlete's
  packages.
- The **Finance** tile shows the **outstanding** total.

## How credits move

A credit is used when a session **finishes**, not when it is booked. A package
sold for one session type only covers sessions of that type — group credits
pay for group sessions, one-on-one credits for one-on-ones.

## Paying

Payment is arranged with your coach directly. Team Assist records what is owed
and what has been paid; it does not take payments itself.
