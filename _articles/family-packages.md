## On Home

- The **Packages** tile shows how many credits are left across all of the
  athlete's packages — for example "75 credits left" — and a badge with the
  number of packages.
- The **Finance** tile shows the **outstanding** total — for example
  "$500.00 outstanding".
- **Needs Attention** carries each open **payment request**: what it is for,
  who sent it, the amount and the due date — for example "$50.00 · Due Sep 6".
  A package with no credits left shows up here too.

## Packages

Tap the **Packages** tile. Packages are grouped by coach or trainer, with a
count next to each name. Every package card shows:

- the package name and **how many credits are used** — "12 of 12 used";
- a pill with credits **left** — "0 left";
- which coach or trainer it belongs to;
- **Paid**, or **Unpaid** with the amount due — "Unpaid · $250.00 due";
- a warning when it is used up: "No credits remaining in this package."

## Finance

Tap the **Finance** tile. The screen shows:

- **Outstanding Balance** at the top — the total you still owe.
- **Active Package** — the package currently being drawn on, with its credits
  remaining and whether it is paid.
- **Payment Requests** — anything your coach has asked you to pay, or "No
  payment requests yet."
- **Payment History** — every payment and credit recorded, newest first, each
  with the amount, how it was paid and the date. A paid request shows a green
  check.

## How credits move

A credit is used when a session **finishes**, not when it is booked. A package
sold for one session type only covers sessions of that type — group credits
pay for group sessions, one-on-one credits for one-on-ones.

## Paying

Payment is arranged with your coach directly. Team Assist records what is owed
and what has been paid; it does not take payments itself. When your coach marks
a request paid, it leaves Needs Attention and appears under Payment History.
