## Bookable sessions

Some sessions are open for booking rather than assigned. The card shows how
many spots are open — for example "2 of 6 open" — and offers **Claim spot**
and **Pass** instead of Accept and Decline.

## Claiming

Tap **Claim spot**. The card shows your claim, and the coach sees who claimed.
The open-spots count goes down for everyone else.

## After you answer

On the **Sessions** sheet (Home › Upcoming › **View all**) a session you
claimed reads **Booked** with a green check, and one you passed on reads
**Passed**. Both stay listed under Past Sessions once the time has gone by.

## Full

When every spot is taken the card reads **Full** and the claim button is gone.
