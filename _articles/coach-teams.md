As a coach, your roster lives on the **Teams** tab.

## The Teams page

Four tiles read your roster at a glance: **Active** players, how many **Teams**, how many
families have **Access** (connected), and your **Staff**. Below them a switch flips the
list between players, **Teams**, and **Staff**, with **Add Athlete** next to the list.

Tap a player to open their page: their team, **Active** / **Connected**, attendance, and
**Quick Actions** — Assignments, Checklists, Message, Info, Progress, and **Access**,
where their family's invite lives.

## Adding a player

Tap **Add Athlete**, type the player's name, a parent's name and phone, then **Save**.
Nothing else is required; a player with no family connected still works for practices,
games and attendance. Have a whole roster in a spreadsheet? See
[adding a team from a spreadsheet]({{ '/teamassist/coach-import-csv/' | relative_url }}).

## Teams

Teams are how practices and games find their players: pick the team in the session
builder and everyone on it comes along. A player has a primary team and can be on others
too.

## Assistant coaches

Switch the list to **Staff** and tap **Add New Assistant Coach**. To give them the team's
practices, games, announcements and chat on their own phone, send them an invite from
**Team Assist Access** — see
[what a staff member sees]({{ '/teamassist/staff-connections/' | relative_url }}).

## Removing someone

Set a player **Inactive** to take them off your active list; that's reversible.
**Delete Athlete** (under **Danger Zone**) is permanent: it removes the record, its
attendance and requests, and takes them off their sessions. Team Assist keeps a backup
snapshot first, restorable from **Tools → Settings → Backup**.
