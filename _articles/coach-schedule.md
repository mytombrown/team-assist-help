Practices and games are built in the **Schedule** tab with the session builder.

## Creating a practice or game

1. Open the **Schedule** tab, tap **Builder**, then **Open Session Builder**.
2. **Session type:** pick **Practice** or **Game** and give it a name.
3. **Date & time.**
4. **Location.**
5. **People:** pick the **team**. Its players come with it.
6. **Playbook:** attach one if you want a plan to run from, or skip it.
7. **Review**, then **Create**.

**Repeating.** The **Repeat** choice is right in the builder: Does Not Repeat, Daily,
Weekdays (Mon–Fri), Weekly, Monthly, or Custom. Team Assist creates the whole series up
front (up to 30) and every family sees all of it at once.

## Seeing the whole day

**Schedule → Sessions** lists every practice and game for the day, plus a **Past · last
30 days** section. Home's Today list is only a summary.

## Editing and rescheduling

Open the practice or game and change its date or time. **Moving it keeps its length**;
to make it longer or shorter, change the end time.

To move it somewhere else, tap **Location**. For a repeating series you choose **this
one only** or **this and the upcoming ones**. Families see the new place right away.

## Cancelling

Deleting always asks first. For a series you choose **Cancel This Session** or **Cancel
All in Series**. **Cancel and Message Group** also pre-fills a text to the team. A
cancelled practice leaves the list straight away, and Team Assist keeps a backup
snapshot you can restore from **Tools → Settings → Backup**.

## Who sees it

Every connected family on the team sees it automatically, and loses it when you delete
it. See [what families see]({{ '/teamassist/coach-families/' | relative_url }}).

## Your iPhone's Calendar

If you allow calendar access, practices and games also go into your iPhone's Calendar
and stay in step when they change.
