Team chat sometimes shows an orange banner at the top of a conversation when messages
are taking longer than usual to send or receive. There are two kinds, and they mean
different things.

## "Team Assist keeps retrying"

The most common banner reads:

> "Some messages aren't syncing yet. Team Assist keeps retrying — if this persists,
> re-enter your invite code to reconnect this device."

Sometimes a **"Retry needed — tap to retry"** banner appears instead, with a short note
about what's waiting.

**What it doesn't mean:** it doesn't mean your messages are gone. Anything you've sent
is saved on your device and delivered once things recover — that's true even if you
close the app or restart your iPhone; pending messages are still there when you come
back.

**What to do:** usually nothing. Give it a moment and it clears on its own. Tapping a
"tap to retry" banner (or the refresh button, or pulling down on the conversation)
prompts another attempt right away. Checking that you have a working internet
connection is worth doing first — the banner can't tell the difference between a
problem at our end and your phone being on a hotel Wi-Fi that hasn't finished
connecting.

## "This device is no longer connected"

The second banner is different:

> "This device is no longer connected for messaging. Re-enter your invite code to
> reconnect."

This one won't clear on its own, because the connection itself needs re-establishing
rather than retrying. Reconnect the same way you first connected: enter the original
invite code and your phone number again. If you no longer have the code, ask the coach
to resend the invite — it's the same code.

## If it won't go away

If the retrying banner stays up for a long stretch on a good connection, that's worth
[reporting]({{ '/report-a-problem/' | relative_url }}) with rough times, which makes it
much easier to trace.
